# land-pr attribution

The diff check before filing, the bad and good title and body examples, the secret check, the
advice to open a real PR instead of a draft, and the REST fallback in § 8 and § 9 are adapted from
the `file-pr` skill in [tstack](https://github.com/t3dotgg/tstack).
Its closing "Created with" attribution line is left out, because this repository allows none.

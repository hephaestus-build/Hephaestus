Workspaces can accept access requests with verified linked accounts, explicit policy acknowledgements, and administrator-approved end dates. Pending renewals do not extend access, and expired access stops authorizing immediately. Request notifications use the configured mail service without including submitted comments in email. Account exports include applicants' own submissions, and account erasure removes them.

Developers can apply and request renewals from a workspace access page. Workspace owners can configure required accounts and notices, while administrators can review applications and inspect email delivery from Access. Optional workspace retention removes completed submissions after their retention window without deleting shared accounts or consent history.

Workspace admins can switch off check-ins and updates from Hephaestus for their workspace in **Workspace settings**, under **Messages from Hephaestus**. Answers, results that people ask for, the weekly digest and feedback on the work are not affected. Operators can turn them off for the whole instance with the Helm value `config.startedMessagesEnabled`.

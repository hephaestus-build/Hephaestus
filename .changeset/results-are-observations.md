What a practice review records about your work is now called an observation instead of a result, in the web app, the Chrome extension, Heph's answers and the docs. For example, practice reviews have an **Observations** tab, the extension lists **Your observations**, and an outdated one is marked **Historical observation**. What a review proposed before Hephaestus checked it is a candidate observation: a practice script's run now says **Returned a candidate observation**, and refused ones are reported as withheld candidate observations.

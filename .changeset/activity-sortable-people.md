Workspace activity lists everyone who contributed in one table that you can sort by each count. It sorts by Contributions first: pull requests opened, pull requests reviewed, and issues opened. A sorted table shows each person's position. Choose 30 days, 90 days, 12 months, all time, or a custom range, then pick a team and repositories. The address keeps your view, so you can share it. Highlights name first contributions and the reviewers who helped the most people. Bot accounts appear apart under Automation, and a workspace admin can treat a machine user account as automation or count it as a person again. Open a person to see their weekly bars, their counts in each repository, and their work. Your own Activity page gets the same ranges and weekly bars.

A review that composed feedback now delivers it. The composing step could end with nothing written
when one unit in a call had a field too long or in the wrong place, which discarded every unit in the
call without a word to the review; when the step's own instructions did not fit beside everything the
review had read; and when the review kept re-sending one refused opening line until its time ran
out. Each unit is now stored or answered on its own, the review makes room before it composes, a
review that wrote nothing while a practice needs a decision is asked once more, a review that keeps
running the same command is moved on, and every refused tool call is in the transcript with its
reason.

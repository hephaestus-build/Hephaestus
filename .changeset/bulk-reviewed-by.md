Workspace admins can change who reviews many practices at once. On **Review**, select practices, then choose **Change selected practices → Reviewed by** and pick Hephaestus, People or Nobody. People asks once for the reason, which every selected practice records. Each practice keeps its read sources, practice script and scan settings, so choosing Hephaestus again later restores how it was reviewed. A practice that changed meanwhile is left as it is, and the summary says how many changed.

Get started now waits in the bottom-right corner of your workspace pages, with a count of the steps you have done. Select it to see what is left before your first practice review. On your first visit the list opens once by itself, without blocking the page or taking your place, and the guided tour no longer opens over the Practice profile. A learn step opens its tour chapter from any page, and the list comes back when the chapter ends. Hide the list at any time; **Get started** in the account menu shows it again. The **Ask Hephaestus** button moved to the header, where its dot still shows a new answer.

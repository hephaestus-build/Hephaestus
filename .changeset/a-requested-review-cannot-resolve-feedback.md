On your Practice profile, your standing, your trend, and the resolution of feedback by your work count only reviews that run on their own. A review that you request and a Past work campaign still show their results. They do not replace the result of an earlier review of the same work. Thus, a review that you request cannot resolve feedback on its own. If only such reviews judged a practice, the practice shows a standing from them and says so.

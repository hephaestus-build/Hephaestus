"hephaestus": patch

A review result that cites nothing and rests only on the sources it checked, such as a practice that does not apply to the changed paths, now shows its text on a developer's practice pages and in practice reviews. Before, its text was hidden and the review said that source access could not be verified. Its text is now hidden only when a source that it rests on is refused, and it is still never sent as new feedback.

A developer's practice pages and practice standings also load their review history faster: the history read no longer loads each result's evidence. Each result records the sources that it rests on when it is written, and the upgrade records them for earlier results, one page at a time.

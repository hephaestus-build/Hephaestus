The upgrade records on each existing Hephaestus turn and weekly digest run the account that owns its conversation and the question or digest that it answers.
An erasure now deletes a person's turns before their questions, so an erasure that stops between the two steps leaves no turn without its question.

**Operators:** Stop every role before this upgrade. Follow **Stop every role before conversation runs record their owner** in the upgrade guide.

**Needs you** on Activity now shows GitHub review requests to a team you are in under **Waiting on others → Requested from your team**, each naming the team it asks ("via payments"), without counting them as yours. On GitLab, when an author asks for your review again after you approved or requested changes, the merge request moves back to **Review requested** instead of staying under **Reviewed by you**. A review request the author sends you again is no longer folded under **Covered by other reviewers** because of your own earlier approval; only another reviewer's approval or request for changes covers it.

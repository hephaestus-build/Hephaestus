Choose once whether your work may use in-house AI, approved cloud AI, or no AI for practice reviews and Heph. Your answer applies across your workspaces and can change in User settings. Workspace setup compares each answer's benefits and trade-offs and shows which models are ready there. Where declared, it distinguishes a model's maker, the service that receives its requests, and who operates the model. Workspace owners can ask members to choose on their first visit and can set required account links separately.

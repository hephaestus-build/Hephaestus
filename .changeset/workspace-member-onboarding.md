"hephaestus": minor
---

Developers choose which AI may handle their work once, for their account, and the answer applies in every workspace they belong to. The three answers are In-house (only systems your organisation runs), Cloud (in-house, plus the providers your organisation approved) and No AI. The answer is a ceiling. Anything stricter also counts, and Hephaestus never switches a developer to anything looser. An answer a workspace has not set up yet can still be chosen. Nothing runs for that purpose there until a workspace owner adds a model, and the page says so. Joining another workspace never asks again, and neither does a workspace adding, removing or re-declaring models. The choice can be changed at any time from **Your AI choice** in the sidebar of any workspace or in **User settings**, and it is part of the account export and removed with account deletion.

Admins declare one fact per model, who operates it (your organisation, or a provider under terms your organisation accepted), and confirm that its terms rule out training. Hephaestus derives the model's tier from it, In-house or Cloud, and shows it as a badge wherever the model appears. An admin-only note holds the region, agreement, renewal date and retention details. Workspace owners assign one model per purpose to each of the In-house, Cloud and Members who haven't chosen rows. A model is refused for a row whose tier it is not declared as.

Member setup is one page in the same voice as sign-in setup. Heph explains what the choice changes, the three answers sit side by side on equal cards, and required Slack or Outline account links sit below. The owner-written welcome text is gone. First-visit setup is optional and starts hidden. Turning it on asks only members who have not answered yet and requires a choice before new personal AI activity. Turning it off again never clears choices or restores AI permission. A member who skipped setup sees the page again only after the owner changes it.

Existing models upgrade with nothing declared, and existing assignments keep serving members who have not chosen until an admin declares who operates each model and assigns models per tier.

"hephaestus": minor
---

Developers choose which AI may handle their work in each workspace: only in-house, in-house or a provider that keeps nothing after the reply, any AI the workspace set up, or no AI. The answer is a ceiling — anything stricter also counts, and Hephaestus never switches a developer to anything looser. An answer the workspace has not set up yet can still be chosen; nothing runs for that purpose until a workspace owner adds a model, and the page says so. The choice can be changed at any time from **Your AI choice** in the sidebar.

Admins declare how each model handles data with two facts — who operates the systems and whether anything is kept after the reply — and confirm that its terms rule out training. Hephaestus derives one data-handling tier from the facts and shows it as a badge wherever the model appears. Workspace owners assign one model per purpose and tier; a model is refused for a row whose tier it is not declared as.

Member setup is one page in the same voice as sign-in setup: Heph explains what the choice changes, the four answers are presented equally, and required Slack or Outline account links sit below. The owner-written welcome text is gone. First-visit setup is optional and starts hidden; turning it on requires members to choose before new personal AI activity, and turning it off again never clears choices or restores AI permission.

Existing models upgrade with no data handling declared, and existing assignments keep serving members who have not chosen until an admin declares the facts and assigns models per tier.

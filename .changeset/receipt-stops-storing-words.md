"hephaestus": patch
---

Hephaestus no longer keeps a second copy of the words that its answers showed for a piece of feedback. The upgrade clears the copies already stored, so the export for a data request no longer contains them, and once an answer is erased its words are gone from those copies too. Feedback that the 1.0 upgrade brought over from an earlier version keeps its words, because it has no answer to read them from. A self-service data export that was ready to download expires during the upgrade if it held such feedback, so you request a new one.

The upgrade rolls, and you do not need to do anything.

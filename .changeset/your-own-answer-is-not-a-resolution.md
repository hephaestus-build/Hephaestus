A feedback card that you mark as addressed or not applicable now shows **Marked by you** on a neutral card, not the green of a resolution. Only your work resolves a card, and the card says that your next work confirms your answer. If you think that a card is wrong, you can now select **Disagree** beside **Not helpful** and say why. Workspace admins read your sentence.

Feedback on your commits now looks forward. It names the pattern, gives one better subject from your own change, and asks for that form in your next commits. While your pull or merge request is open, or where your repository squash-merges, it points you to the title or squash message. It no longer asks you to reword, rebase, squash or force-push commits you already pushed. A committed secret stays the one exception: purge it from history and rotate it.

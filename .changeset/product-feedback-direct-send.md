Send feedback opens the same form directly on every screen size. Describe the problem or idea without choosing a category. Page details are optional, and incomplete forms explain what is missing. A short receipt confirms a saved report and links to Your reports, also available in the account menu.

General feedback can become a public issue when it describes an actionable problem, you allow sharing, and the privacy checks pass. Ordinary accounts can submit up to five reports per UTC day across workspaces. Instance admins are exempt from this daily cap. Existing pacing and drafting limits still apply.

Maintainer publication keeps the checks for a clear, actionable issue in force. A short notice confirms when you keep a report private.

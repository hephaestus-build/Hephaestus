The practice panel opens with an introduction: why the practice matters, what good work looks like and, where the practice has them, a picture of the idea and a longer guide behind **Read more**. The tabs **Observations** and **Feedback** follow it, and **Observations** starts with **Where you stand**. You can hide the introduction of a practice that you know. The group panel drops its tabs: its description, **Where you stand** and its practices show in one column. Heph explains a practice in the same words as its panel. "Keep the change reviewable" is the first Hephaestus default with a picture and a guide.

Workspace and instance admins can add a **Visual** and a **Guide** to a practice. Each picture is an SVG file of shapes and text that can take its colors from the light or dark theme.

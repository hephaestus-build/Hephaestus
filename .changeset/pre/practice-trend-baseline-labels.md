Trends now read **Increasing baseline**, **Decreasing baseline**, **No clear baseline change** and **No baseline yet**. A trend's sentence first says in plain words whether your recent work meets the practice more or less often than before. For a practice, it then says what it compares: your last four reviewed pieces of work with the four before them. For a group, it compares practice by practice. Without a trend yet, the sentence says what a trend needs and promises nothing more.

Every link that opens a new tab now shows the outbound icon and tells screen readers "(opens in a new tab)". This includes the header, the footer, the about and landing pages, contributor and repository cards, the GitLab token help, and links in your instance's privacy notice and imprint. In the instance catalog, a screen reader now hears "Saving" and the group or practice name when a change takes longer than a second.

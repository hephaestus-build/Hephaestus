Workspace admins can change who reviews many practices at once. On **Review settings**, under **Feedback mode**, select practices, then choose **Change selected practices → Reviewed by** and pick Hephaestus, People or Nobody. People asks once for the reason, which every selected practice records. Each practice keeps its read sources, practice script and scan settings, so choosing Hephaestus again later restores how it was reviewed. Each change saves a new version, so an earlier reason stays in the version history. A practice that Hephaestus cannot review, or that changed meanwhile, stays as it was, and the summary names it.

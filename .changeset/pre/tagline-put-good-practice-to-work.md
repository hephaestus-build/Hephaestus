Hephaestus has a new tagline, *Put good practice to work.*, and a clearer description of what it does: it helps your team put good engineering practices into everyday work, with feedback on the work, in a weekly digest, and on your Practice profile. You see it on the sign-in page, in link previews, in the Slack app home, and in the documentation.

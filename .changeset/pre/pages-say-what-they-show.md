Admin pages now say what they do not show. A running review of past work is always found, even in a workspace with many earlier ones, and the page says when older ones are not listed. The review scope lists every member, so a person past the first 100 can be chosen and its count of chosen people stays right. An audit log export over 10,000 events now tells you that the file holds only the newest ones. A link to a conversation message further back than the page reads no longer says the message may have been deleted.

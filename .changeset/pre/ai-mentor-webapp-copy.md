Hephaestus now introduces itself as the open-source AI mentor for software engineering practices, with the tagline *Get better at the work you already do.* The start page, the About page, link previews and the installed app say what it does: it checks your work against your team's practices, says what to improve and why when your work gives it grounds, and stays silent when it does not. The start page also answers who sees your feedback.

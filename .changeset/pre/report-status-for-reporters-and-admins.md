**Your reports** now shows who can read each report (Private, Can become public, or Public) and a status line with when you sent it, the issue it became, and how that issue was closed. Allow public is now a small switch on the report, and the page explains it once, with a link to your default in your settings. Instance admins see the same on **Product feedback**: whether the reporter allows a public issue, when a held report becomes public, and the model's draft while it waits. The admin card also puts the sender and workspace on their own line, and the browser details wrap.

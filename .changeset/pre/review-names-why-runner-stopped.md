A review attempt that fails without a result now says why on its job, without the review sandbox's log: for example that no practice produced a result, that the attempt's time ran out before the review started, or which kind of error stopped the review sandbox. When Hephaestus answers a review's results with its own error, the job names the step that failed and the kind of cause. When no practice was evaluated and no model was called, the job says that too.

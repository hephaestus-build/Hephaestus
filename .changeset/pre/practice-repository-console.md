Practice setup now shows which practices are edited in the practice repository. Such a practice is read-only there: you cannot edit, delete, or reset it, move it to another group, or accept a catalog update for it. You can still change its feedback mode and its position in its group. Its panel names the repository and, when its current version was read from there, the commit. **Edit in Hephaestus** makes it editable in Hephaestus again after you confirm. The history of a practice in Practice reviews shows the commit that each version from the repository was read at.

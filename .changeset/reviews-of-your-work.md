The Practice profile's **Latest run** chip now opens **Reviews of your work**: every review that ran
on your own work, newest first and grouped by day, each heading naming its day. A row leads with the
practices the review marked as problems, by name, counts what the review reached under it, and where
the review left feedback on the work, **Read the feedback on the pull request** opens it where it was
left. The newest review wears a filled chip, a review that is not the first on its work says which
one it is, and reading to the end of the list loads the earlier reviews. Opening a review shows the
work it ran on, by the name that work has now rather than the one it had when the review ran, and
everything it recorded about you under its practice group, with the way to the feedback that carried
it. The list narrows by kind of work and by timeframe, and where you may ask for another review of
your own work, **Review this now** is one press away. A review has two tabs: **Every practice**, what each practice
saw about your work or why it stayed quiet, filterable by group, by the event that starts a review
and by name, and **What we noticed**, everything recorded about the work.
Only you can see the list.

**Review activity** is no longer listed in the sidebar; the practice profile's **Latest run** chip is
the way to the reviews of your work, and the pages stay reachable by their address.

A piece of feedback that closed because its practice's review rules changed now reads as closed rather
than as resolved: it drops the count of clean work, which nothing can complete any more, and the tab
over the cards is called **Resolved and closed**.

You can now follow your product feedback after you send it. When your instance files product feedback on GitHub, the first report asks whether it may become a public issue, and later reports show the choice as a switch for that report. After you send, the dialog says where the report is going, and a public report waits 5 minutes so you can keep it private or edit the drafted issue. **Your product feedback** in the header menu lists your reports, links their public issues, and lets you make a public issue private or allow a private one to become public. User settings has a **Product feedback** section for your choice and for an opt-in mention of your GitHub account. Instance admins set the repositories, the drafting model, the daily draft limit and a pause switch under **Admin → Product feedback**, see their health, and retry reports that did not file. The header menu no longer links to the Hephaestus issue tracker.

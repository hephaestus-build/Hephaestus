Product surveys are now invitations in the header rather than automatic popups. You can leave and resume a draft, skip optional questions, and undo an accidental decline. Product feedback includes the current page path only when you choose to share it, and failed submissions keep your draft with an explanation.

Instance administrators can publish multi-question surveys, schedule collection, and pause or resume invitations without changing existing answers. Responses stay on the instance; no analytics or browser storage is added.

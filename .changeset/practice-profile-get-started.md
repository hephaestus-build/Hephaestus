While a setup step is still open, your Practice profile starts with **Get started**. It lists those steps, such as your AI choice, the accounts your workspace needs, and the review itself, then ways to learn the page. Each step comes from your real state: it is done only when it has happened, and the list goes away once every step is done. Select its heading to hide it. While a step is open, **Get started** under **You** in the sidebar opens it from any page.

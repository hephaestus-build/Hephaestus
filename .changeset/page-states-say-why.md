Pages that cannot load now say why and offer the next step. A missing page or one you cannot access takes you to the home page, and only a failed load offers Retry. Pages show their outline while they load, and the sign-in return page says that it is signing you in. When Heph cannot be shown, a message takes its place instead of Heph disappearing. It offers Retry, or Reload page when Heph did not load, and you can close it.

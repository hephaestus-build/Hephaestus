A review whose sandbox cannot prepare its workspace for a reason that every attempt would meet now stops at once as a preparation failure. Such reasons are a workspace archive that exceeds its budget or that cannot be extracted, or code changes that cannot be shown. The review keeps the results that it already has. Before, the review ran again until it had used all its attempts, and it then reported a failed execution. A workspace download that a restart or network failure cuts still runs again.

Developers can open **Across the workspace** under their Practice profile to see their own figures and their standing in each practice group beside how the workspace's developers split across it, counted in developers and never named. Each figure shows the middle half of the developers, from the 25th to the 75th percentile, including how much feedback is open. A group opens its practices, each compared on its own, and every comparison leads back to the developer's own group or practice on their Practice profile. A split appears only once every part of it, those with no standing there yet included, holds at least five other developers.

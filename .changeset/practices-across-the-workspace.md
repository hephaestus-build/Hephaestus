Developers can open **Across the workspace** under their Practice profile to see their own figures and their standing in each practice group beside how the workspace's developers split across it, counted in developers and never named. The range shows the last 30 days, the last 90 days or all time. Each figure shows the middle half of the developers with a standing once six others have one, while open feedback is counted now over every developer in the workspace, reviewed or not. A group opens its practices, each compared on its own, and the developer's own group and practices open over it with their own observations and feedback. A split appears only once each of its parts holds at least four developers, so every reader sees the same split and each part stands for at least three others; otherwise the bar is held back.

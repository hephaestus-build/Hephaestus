Developers can open **Across the workspace** under their Practice profile to see their own figures and their standing in each practice group beside how the workspace's developers split across it, counted in developers and never named. The range shows the last 30 days, the last 90 days or all time. Each figure shows the middle half of the developers here once ten other developers are observed, and open feedback is counted now for everyone. A group opens its practices, each compared on its own, and a practice opens over its group with the developer's own observations and feedback; the group leads on to their Practice profile. A part of a split appears only once it holds at least six developers, so every reader sees the same split and each part stands for at least five others.

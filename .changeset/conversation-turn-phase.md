A question to Hephaestus now tells the app more about where it stands. While Hephaestus answers, the app can tell whether it is still starting or already working on the answer. It also knows how many attempts a question gets. When an answer fails, the app learns why: the question waited too long to start, Hephaestus ran out of attempts, the model stopped answering, or the person's data was erased while Hephaestus answered.

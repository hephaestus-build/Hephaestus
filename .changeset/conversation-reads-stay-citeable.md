In a long conversation, Hephaestus no longer summarises the conversation in the middle of an answer, unless that one answer outgrows the AI model's context on its own. The summary now happens before your next question. Before, a summary in the middle of an answer could replace the files that Hephaestus had just read. Hephaestus also says when it has not read a test or the code that a change calls, instead of describing them.

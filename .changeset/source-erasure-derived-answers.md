A Slack disconnect or a Slack channel erasure now also erases the Hephaestus answers that used the erased Slack messages, and the answers that build on them. As with a person's erasure, this includes every answer that came after a file read in the same conversation. When a disconnect deletes the practice results behind feedback that Hephaestus showed in a conversation, the answers that showed it are erased too, unless the results only reached their retention. A person's erasure of Outline content also erases the later answers that read an erased copy.

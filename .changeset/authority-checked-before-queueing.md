A Slack reply, an email or a comment on the work now keeps the access that its request was checked under. If someone leaves a workspace while an answer runs or while a send is admitted, that reply or send does not go out. An approval from an admin who just lost the role is refused. Withdrawing a comment still works after access narrowed. A review whose developer turns AI off while its next model call waits does not send that call.

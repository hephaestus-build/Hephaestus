A review attempt that fails without a result now says why on its job, without the sandbox log: for example that no practice produced a result, that the attempt's time ran out before the review started, or which kind of error stopped the runner. When the server refuses a review's results before any practice was evaluated or any model was called, the job now says so, instead of only that the server answered with its own error.

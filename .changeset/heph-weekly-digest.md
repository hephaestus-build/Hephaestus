Hephaestus can add a weekly digest to your Heph conversation. Turn it on in **User settings**. Each Monday from 09:00 in your time zone, Hephaestus adds a digest of the week before to your conversation in each workspace where Heph can answer you. It lists the practice feedback that reached you and what changed on your Practice profile. It also counts the new pieces of feedback that wait for you. Quiet weeks send nothing.

A short note in Slack tells you that the digest is ready. Without a connected Slack account, you get an email instead. If Hephaestus cannot reach you either way, the digest is only in your conversation. The note contains no feedback. Reply in the digest's thread to go through it with Heph. The setting **Comments and Slack reminders** is now **Comments on your work**, and the Slack reminder about prepared feedback is gone.

A developer's practice page now shows all of their feedback. It stopped at the newest 20 cards, so an older open card could disappear, and the tab counts and the weekly digest read too low. Counts can rise after this upgrade, because they are now right. Review schedules that fall due together now open in the same tick, instead of 20 every five minutes. In a conversation, Hephaestus now knows when the team has more Outline documents or Slack threads than it was shown, so it does not treat the missing ones as absent.

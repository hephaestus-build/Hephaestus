On your Practice profile, your standing, your trend, and the resolution of feedback by your work count only reviews that run on their own. A review that you request and a Past work campaign still show their results in your reviews. They do not replace the result of an earlier review of the same work in your standing, your trend, or the resolution of feedback. Thus, a review that you request cannot resolve feedback on its own. If only such reviews judged a practice, the practice shows a standing from them and says so. When a practice reads **Needs attention** but your newest review of it is one you requested that found no problem, its **Observations** tab says that a requested review is evidence only.

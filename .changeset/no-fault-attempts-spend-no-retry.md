"hephaestus": minor
---

A practice review no longer spends a retry for a reason that is not its own. When a worker's drain stops it, for example during an upgrade, it runs again on another worker without spending a retry; after 3 drains, the next one cancels it. On Kubernetes, a review whose Pod the sandbox quota refuses waits in the queue instead of failing after two minutes. Queued reviews end with "Hephaestus had no room to run this review in time." only when they have waited for an hour while the quota had no room for one and no review ran, for example because Heph's sandboxes held it; waiting behind running reviews never ends a review. A quota smaller than one review sandbox cancels the queued reviews at once with "The sandbox quota is smaller than one review sandbox." A scan ends with the same reason instead of queueing its next file. The metrics `agent_job_drained_total`, `sandbox_start_failures_total` and `sandbox_review_starved_seconds` show these. The worker needs `get` and `list` on `events` and `resourcequotas` in the sandbox namespace, which the chart's Role grants; a Role of your own needs them too. API clients can now receive the new `NO_SANDBOX_ROOM` stop reason on reviews and scans; a client that maps stop reasons must accept it. During the rolling upgrade to this release, a worker of the previous release can count a review's earlier no-fault attempts as retries once.

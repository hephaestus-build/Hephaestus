Sign in without losing your place: public pages now open a dismissible sign-in dialog, with a full-page fallback for shared links and reloads. That page tells you what to expect before you commit — what Hephaestus does, and the two setup steps that follow. Privacy and imprint links are on every sign-in and setup screen, so they are reachable even though those screens carry no site footer. A stalled session check no longer prevents you from returning to the sign-in form.

First-time setup is now two clearly numbered steps. Step one shows the data notice and the terms of use and asks you to accept them. Step two asks separately whether you want to take part in the research, explains what that means in three short points, and gives equal, unselected buttons for taking part and continuing without it. You can go back to the notice, sign out, or change your research choice later in settings. Failed notice loads and saves can be retried without entering a broken workspace or accepting an outdated notice.

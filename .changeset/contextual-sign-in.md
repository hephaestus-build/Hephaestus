Sign in without losing your place: public pages now open a dismissible sign-in dialog, with a full-page fallback for shared links and reloads. Privacy information is available before choosing a provider, including on instances with only one sign-in option. A stalled session check no longer prevents you from returning to the sign-in form.

First-time setup separates terms and privacy acknowledgement from an optional research invitation. Choose to participate or continue without research, revisit the notice, and change your research choice later in settings. Failed notice loads and saves can be retried without entering a broken workspace or accepting an outdated notice.

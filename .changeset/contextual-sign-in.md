Sign in without losing your place: public pages now open a dismissible sign-in dialog, with a full-page fallback for shared links and reloads. Privacy and imprint links are on every sign-in and setup screen, so they are reachable even though those screens carry no site footer. A stalled session check no longer prevents you from returning to the sign-in form.

First-time setup is now a single page that shows both of its decisions at once, instead of a two-step wizard. The data notice and the terms of use come first, with one box to accept them. Underneath, the research question stands on its own: a short summary of why it matters, what you would share and what it asks of you, then two equally weighted choices — take part, or don't. Neither is selected for you, both cost the same click, and you can change the research answer later in settings. Nothing is recorded until you press Continue.

Sign-in and setup also look like the rest of Hephaestus now, rather than like a form: the same headings, cards and brand colour the app uses once you are inside it.

# Product feedback boundaries

Product feedback collects identified submissions for instance administrators.
It is separate from practice feedback and stays on the instance.
The [admin guide](/admin/product-feedback) and [user guide](/user/product-feedback) describe the controls.
The [personal-data map](/admin/dsms/personal-data-map) defines erasure ownership.
The code is `server/application/src/main/java/de/tum/cit/aet/hephaestus/productfeedback/`.

## Boundaries

- Workspace endpoints require membership and derive the account from authentication.
  Instance endpoints also accept submissions from accounts without a workspace.
  Instance admins own inbox access and can resolve or reopen a submission.
- Drafts live in the root route’s product feedback controls (`__root.tsx`).
  A reload, workspace switch, or sign-out removes them.
  `use-product-feedback.ts` submits through the generated client.
- The unique constraint on `(account_id, submission_minute)` limits product feedback to one submission per minute.
- Research consent belongs to `ConsentService` and `ResearchParticipationQuery`.
  Practice research uses it independently of product feedback.

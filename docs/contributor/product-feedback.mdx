# Product feedback boundaries

Product feedback collects identified submissions for instance administrators. It is separate from
practice feedback and never leaves the instance. What administrators and members see is in the
[operator guidance](/admin/product-feedback) and the [user guide](/user/product-feedback); erasure
ownership is in the [personal-data map](/admin/dsms/personal-data-map). The code is
`server/application/src/main/java/de/tum/cit/aet/hephaestus/productfeedback/`.

## Model

A `Survey` is published once and its questions are not editable afterwards; `Survey.isOpenFor` is the
one home of availability, derived from the schedule, the pause flag and the targeted workspace, and
never stored.

A `SurveyParticipation` is one row per survey and account, keyed by the unique constraint on
`(survey_id, account_id)`: it starts as `INVITED` when the invitation is first shown, becomes
`RESPONDED` or `DECLINED`, and an undone decline returns it to `INVITED`. Answers are stored as a
list of `{questionId, text | choices | rating}`; `SurveyQuestions` validates them against the frozen
questions and produces the per-question summary.

`ProductFeedback` carries the message, the context the sender opted into, the release that received
it and inbox triage. Names next to any record come from the `AccountSummaryQuery` and
`WorkspaceSummaryQuery` SPIs, one batch per page.

## Boundaries

- Workspace endpoints require membership and derive the account from authentication; availability
  and workspace targeting are checked on every write. Instance administrator endpoints own
  authoring, lifecycle, results, export and the inbox.
- The webapp owns drafts and the invitation cache (`use-product-feedback.ts`); the generated client
  is the only HTTP boundary. A response or decline is handled once per account, not once per
  workspace.
- Feedback is rate-limited by the unique constraint on `(account_id, submission_minute)`.

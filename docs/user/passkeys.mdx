# Passkeys

A passkey lets your device confirm that it is you.
Hephaestus still uses your connected sign-in provider to identify your account.
A passkey adds protection before you use admin access.

Your instance or workspace can require passkeys for administration.
You cannot turn off a required policy through your personal settings.
You can still use your ordinary member access without admin verification.

## Add a passkey

1. Sign in and open **User settings**.
2. Find **Passkeys** and enter a name for your device.
3. Select **Register passkey** and follow your browser's instructions.
4. Select **Verify passkey** and follow the verification prompt.
5. Add a second passkey on another device.
6. Select **Replace recovery codes** and store the codes in a safe place outside the browser.

Your device can use its screen lock, fingerprint, or face recognition to confirm access.
Hephaestus does not receive that biometric data.
Use a current browser over a secure connection.

## Confirm admin access

Select **Verify passkey** in **User settings** when Hephaestus asks you to confirm access.
Then return to the admin page and repeat your action.
Hephaestus does not repeat a refused action automatically.
Sensitive changes need a recent verification.

Personal protection applies even when your instance makes passkeys optional.
Turning off personal protection does not remove an instance or workspace requirement.
Verify a passkey before you change personal protection or recovery codes.
You must keep at least one registered passkey.

## Recover access

If you lose your passkeys, sign in through your usual provider and open **Recover passkeys** in **User settings**.
Enter a recovery code and select **Start recovery**.
Recovery removes the old passkeys and signs out your other sessions.
It does not restore admin access.
Register and verify a replacement passkey, add another passkey, and replace your recovery codes.

Each recovery code works once.
Recovery also invalidates the remaining codes.
Replacing the codes makes all earlier codes invalid.
If you have no passkey or recovery code, contact your instance operator.
A workspace admin cannot reset your passkeys.

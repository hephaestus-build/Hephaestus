## Before you start

You need:

- an account with one of the sign-in providers offered by your Hephaestus deployment
- access to at least one workspace
- a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari

The public TUM deployment is available at [hephaestus.build](https://hephaestus.build). Other deployments can offer different sign-in providers and features.

## Sign in

1. Open your Hephaestus deployment.
2. Select **Sign in**.
3. Choose one of the available providers and complete its sign-in flow.
4. Select a workspace from the workspace switcher.

If you do not have a workspace, ask an instance admin to create one, or share your account ID from **User settings** with a workspace owner who can add you. Some deployments also allow eligible users to select **Create workspace** from the workspace switcher.

### Connect an organizational account

If your deployment offers organizational sign-in and you already have a Hephaestus account, sign in
with your existing method first. Open **User settings → Connected Accounts** and choose **Connect**
for your organization. Complete the organization's sign-in flow to attach that identity to your
existing account.

Signing in with an identity you have not connected creates a separate account, even if it has the same
email address. The issuer shown beside a connected account distinguishes different organizations or
realms. Keep at least one available sign-in method connected; Slack and Outline connections are not
sign-in methods. An organizational sign-in alone does not give you access to a private workspace.

### Join and manage workspace access

Workspace access belongs to your Hephaestus account. You do not need a GitHub or GitLab connection to
be a member. Your account ID is shown in **User settings** and stays the same when you connect another
sign-in method. Connect GitHub or GitLab to see your own source-control profile and configure practice
feedback preferences.

In **Workspace admin → Members**, owners and administrators can add an existing account by ID,
change its role, or suspend access. Only owners can grant or remove ownership. Before stepping down
or deleting your account, promote a successor if you are the final owner.

A suspension immediately removes member access and prevents synchronization from restoring it.
Public pages and instance-administrator access are unaffected. Use **Restore
access** to explicitly choose a role and restore the membership. Disconnecting an SCM identity does
not remove manually granted workspace access. Workspace access and provider-side organization
permissions are separate: suspending workspace access does not revoke GitHub or GitLab permissions.

## Find your way around

The navigation changes with the features enabled for your workspace.

| Area | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | View a developer's recent activity and, when enabled, progress and league information. |
| **Teams** | See contributors grouped by team and explore their activity. |
| **Review activity** | Look back over the practice feedback you have received. |
| **Leaderboard** | View the optional weekly ranking of review activity. |
| **Heph** | Start a chat about your feedback and recent work when the mentor is enabled for you. |
| **Administration** | Configure the workspace if you are a workspace admin. |
| **User settings** | Manage your account, linked identities, sessions, and personal preferences. |

Features that are off do not appear in the navigation.

## Try the core workflows

### Read practice feedback

When practice reviews are enabled, Hephaestus reviews eligible pull requests, merge requests, and issues
in connected repositories. When it delivers feedback, the feedback explains the practice it observed,
points to evidence, and suggests a next step. A completed review does not always produce a public comment.

See [Practice feedback](./ai-code-review) for triggers, comment structure, and the personal comment-delivery setting.

### Chat with Heph

Open **Heph**, start a new chat, and ask about a piece of feedback or recent work. Heph can use the issues and pull or merge requests you recently authored, and the feedback you have received, as context.

See [Chat with Heph](./ai-mentor) for examples and privacy notes.

### Switch workspaces

Use the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar. Each workspace has its own repositories, members, practice catalog, integrations, and feature settings.

## Connected services

- **GitHub or GitLab** supplies the repository activity used by the workspace.
- **Slack**, when connected, lets you chat with Heph in a direct message. Workspace admins can separately configure weekly leaderboard digests and monitored channels.
- **Outline**, when connected, lets Hephaestus read selected team documents that are relevant to linked work. Connecting your own Outline account attributes documents you authored to you.

Need help? Use **Report issue** or **Request a feature** in the footer, or ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hephaestus-build/Hephaestus/discussions).

Use this guide to sign in, complete first-visit setup, and choose your first task.

## Before you start

You need:

- an account with one of the sign-in providers offered by your Hephaestus deployment
- access to at least one workspace
- a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari

The public TUM deployment is available at [hephaestus.build](https://hephaestus.build). Other deployments can offer different sign-in providers and features.

## Sign in

1. Open your Hephaestus deployment.
2. Select **Sign in**.
3. Select a provider, such as **Continue with GitHub**.
4. Finish the provider’s sign-in.

If the **Get set up** page appears:

1. Read **Terms and privacy**.
2. Select **I accept the terms of use**.
3. If a research question appears, choose whether to take part.
4. Select **Continue**.

To enter your workspace:

1. Select a workspace from the workspace switcher.
2. If workspace setup appears, choose which AI may handle your work: **In-house**, **Cloud**, or **No AI**.
3. On that page, connect each required account that is available.
4. Select **Save AI choice**.

The workspace opens your **Practice profile** if practice reviews are on, or **Activity** otherwise.

See [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#signing-in-and-choosing-research-participation) for the sign-in notice.
You answer the AI question once. Your answer applies in all your workspaces on this deployment.
To change it, select **Your AI choice** in the sidebar or open **User settings**.
If you select **Skip for now**, **Your AI choice** in the sidebar opens the setup page again.

If you do not have a workspace:

1. Ask an instance admin to create one or add you.

Some deployments also let eligible users select **Create workspace** from the workspace switcher.

## Find your way around

The navigation changes with what is set up in your workspace.

| Area                   | What you can do                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Practice profile**   | See how your work stands on the practices your workspace reviews. A workspace that reviews practices opens here.                                                                          |
| **Activity**           | See what needs you and what you did over a time range, with the work behind it. A workspace that does not review practices opens here.                                                    |
| **Workspace activity** | See what everyone in the workspace, or one team, worked on, member by member.                                                                                                             |
| **Teams**              | See contributors grouped by team.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Heph**               | Talk about feedback and recent work. This works when the workspace has a model within your AI choice. Only workspace members get Heph, so an instance admin who is not a member does not. |
| **Your AI choice**     | Choose In-house, Cloud, or No AI for your account. Connect the accounts this workspace requires.                                                                                          |
| **Instance admin**     | Operate the instance, if you are an instance admin. This does not make you a workspace member.                                                                                            |
| **Administration**     | Configure the workspace if you are a workspace admin.                                                                                                                                     |
| **User settings**      | Manage [your AI choice, connected accounts, notifications, sessions, and account data](./user-settings).                                                                                  |

**Practice profile** appears only when practice reviews are on.
**Heph** appears for workspace members even if its model is not set up yet.
Its page explains why it cannot answer.
[Activity](./activity) explains the two activity pages.
On your Practice profile, the chip beside the title opens [the reviews that recorded something about your work](./practice-profile#reviews-of-your-work).

## Try the core workflows

### Read practice feedback

When practice reviews are on, Hephaestus reviews eligible pull requests, merge requests, and issues in connected repositories.
Each piece of feedback explains the practice, points to evidence, and suggests a next step.
By default, feedback on the work waits for a workspace admin’s approval.
A completed review does not always produce a public comment.

1. Open **Practice profile**.
2. If a review appears beside the title, select it to open reviews of your work.
3. Open a review to read its observations.

Before your first recorded result, the chip says **No review yet** and does not open a list.

See [Practice feedback](./ai-code-review) for triggers, comment structure, and the personal comment-delivery setting.

### Chat with Heph

1. Open **Heph**.
2. Enter a question, such as "What feedback did I get on my latest pull request?"
3. Press Enter to send it.

Heph can use your recent issues, pull or merge requests, and feedback as context.

See [Chat with Heph](./ai-mentor) for examples and privacy limits.
If you chose **No AI**, you can still use Activity and read existing feedback.

### Switch workspaces

1. Select a workspace in the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.

Each workspace has its own repositories, members, practice catalog, integrations, and settings.

## Connected services

- **GitHub or GitLab** supplies the repository activity used by the workspace.
- **Slack**, when connected, lets you chat with Heph in a direct message. Workspace admins can separately configure monitored channels.
- **Outline**, when connected, lets Hephaestus read selected team documents that are relevant to linked work. Your connected Outline account identifies you as the author of your documents.

Select **Feedback** in the header, then **Share an idea**, **Report a bug**, or **Send feedback**.
These reach your instance admins.
The same menu has **Open an issue on GitHub**, for a public issue.
You can also ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hephaestus-build/Hephaestus/discussions).

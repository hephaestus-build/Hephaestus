This tutorial takes you from sign-in to your first feedback.
At the end, you are signed in, your workspace knows your AI choice, and you know where your feedback appears.

## Before you start

You need:

- an account with one of the sign-in providers offered by your Hephaestus deployment
- access to at least one workspace
- a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari

Each deployment chooses its own sign-in providers and features.
The Technical University of Munich runs a public deployment at [hephaestus.build](https://hephaestus.build).

Workspace members come from GitHub or GitLab.
If GitHub or GitLab makes you a member of a workspace, the workspace appears after you sign in with that account.
If you already use Hephaestus with another account, connect that account under [User settings](./user-settings#connected-accounts) instead.
A GitLab workspace with **Selected projects** needs more than group membership. [Create a workspace](./workspace#create-a-workspace) gives the rule.

If you are not a member, ask whoever manages that organization, group, or project to add you.
The workspace appears after the next sync.
To start a workspace for your own team, see [Create a workspace](./workspace#create-a-workspace).

## Sign in

1. Open your Hephaestus deployment.
2. Select **Sign in**.
3. Select a provider, such as **Continue with GitHub**.
4. Finish the provider’s sign-in.

Hephaestus opens the **Get set up** page, or your workspace if you have already accepted the terms.

Your provider identity connects your work to your account.
It does not grant workspace access or AI permission by itself.

## Accept the terms

The **Get set up** page appears on your first sign-in, and again when the terms or the research question change.

1. Read **Terms and privacy**.
2. Select **I accept the terms of use**.
3. If **Allow research use of your data?** appears, choose **Yes, allow research use** or **No, keep my data out of research**.
4. Select **Save and continue**.

Hephaestus continues to the page you opened.
[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#signing-in-and-choosing-research-participation) explains the sign-in notice.

## Choose how AI handles your work

1. Select a workspace from the workspace switcher.
2. If workspace setup appears, choose which AI may handle your work: **In-house**, **Cloud**, or **No AI**.
3. On that page, connect each required account that is available.
4. Select **Save AI choice**.

The workspace opens your **Practice profile** if practice reviews are on, or **Activity** otherwise.

You answer the AI question once.
Your answer applies in all your workspaces on this deployment.
To change it, select **Your AI choice** in the sidebar or open **User settings**.
If you select **Skip for now**, **Your AI choice** in the sidebar opens the setup page again.

The accounts you connect each add context:

- **GitHub or GitLab** supplies the repository activity used by the workspace.
- **Slack**, when connected, lets you talk with Hephaestus in a direct message. Workspace admins can separately configure monitored channels.
- **Outline**, when connected, lets Hephaestus read selected team documents that are relevant to linked work. Your connected Outline account identifies you as the author of your documents.

## Find your way around

The navigation changes with what is set up in your workspace.

| Group | Page | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You** | **Practice profile** | See how your work stands on the practices your workspace reviews. A workspace that reviews practices opens here. |
| **You** | **Activity** | See what needs you and what you did over a time range, with the work behind it. A workspace that does not review practices opens here. |
| **You** | **Ask Hephaestus** | Talk about feedback and recent work. The workspace needs a conversation model within your AI choice. Only workspace members can use this page. |
| **Workspace** | **Practices across the workspace** | See how practices stand across the workspace. |
| **Workspace** | **Workspace activity** | See who contributed to the workspace, or one team, and sort them by each count. |
| **Workspace** | **Teams** | See members grouped by team. |
| **Administration** | Workspace setup pages | Configure the workspace if you are a workspace admin. Use **Team setup** to configure teams. |
| Footer | **Your AI choice** | Choose In-house, Cloud, or No AI for your account. Connect the accounts this workspace requires. |
| Footer | **Instance administration** | Operate the instance, if you are an instance admin. This does not make you a workspace member. |
| Footer | **User settings** | Manage [your AI choice, connected accounts, notifications, sessions, and account data](./user-settings). The account menu in the header also opens it. |
| Header | **Send feedback** | [Send an idea, a bug report, or feedback about Hephaestus](./product-feedback) to the maintainers. |

**Practice profile** and **Practices across the workspace** appear only when practice reviews are on.
**Ask Hephaestus** appears for workspace members even if it has no model.
Its page explains why it cannot answer.

## Switch workspaces

You can belong to several workspaces on one deployment.
Each workspace has its own repositories, members, practices, and settings.

1. Open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.
2. Select a workspace.

Hephaestus keeps you on the same kind of page, such as **Activity**.
A page about one item, such as a person or a practice, has no match there.
Hephaestus then opens the home of the new workspace and says so.

If a link names a workspace that you cannot open, Hephaestus opens another workspace that you can open.
A message says *You cannot open the workspace* and gives its name.
If you can open no workspace, the page says **No workspace**.

## Read your feedback

When practice reviews are on, Hephaestus reviews eligible pull requests, merge requests, and issues in connected repositories.
Each piece of feedback explains the practice, points to evidence, and suggests a next step.
By default, feedback on the work waits for a workspace admin’s approval.
A completed review does not always produce a public comment.

1. Under **You**, open **Practice profile**.
2. Open a card under **Your recurring feedback patterns**.
3. Optional: To see each review of your work, select the chip beside the title.

Before your first review, the chip says **No review yet**. While your first review waits or runs, the chip names the work, and it opens no list.
If you chose **No AI**, you can use Activity and read existing feedback.

## Before you start

You need:

- an account with one of the sign-in providers offered by your Hephaestus deployment
- access to at least one workspace
- a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari

The public TUM deployment is available at [hephaestus.build](https://hephaestus.build). Other deployments can offer different sign-in providers and features.

## Sign in

1. Open your Hephaestus deployment.
2. Select **Sign in**.
3. Choose an available provider.
4. Complete its sign-in process.
5. The first time you sign in, read the data notice.
6. On your first sign-in, accept the terms.
7. If a research question appears, answer it.
8. On your first sign-in, select **Continue**.
9. Select a workspace from the workspace switcher.
10. If asked, choose which AI may handle your work: **In-house**, **Cloud**, or **No AI**.
11. Connect the accounts that the workspace requires.

See [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#signing-in-and-choosing-research-participation) for the sign-in notice.
You answer the AI question once. Your answer applies in all your workspaces.
**Your AI choice** in the sidebar or **User settings** lets you change it.
After **Skip for now**, **Your AI choice** in the sidebar opens the setup page again.

If you do not have a workspace:

1. Ask an instance admin to create one or add you.

Some deployments also let eligible users select **Create workspace** from the workspace switcher.

## Find your way around

The navigation changes with what is set up in your workspace.

| Area | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Practice profile** | See how your work stands on the practices your workspace reviews, when it reviews practices. A workspace that reviews practices opens here. |
| **Activity** | See what needs you and what you did over a time range. See the work you did it on. A workspace that does not review practices opens here. |
| **Workspace activity** | See what everyone in the workspace, or one team, worked on, member by member. |
| **Teams** | See contributors grouped by team. |
| **Heph** | For workspace members only. Talk about feedback and recent work, within your AI choice, when the workspace has a model within your choice. An instance admin who is not a member gets no Heph in that workspace. |
| **Your AI choice** | Choose In-house, Cloud, or No AI for your account. Connect the accounts this workspace requires. |
| **Instance admin** | Operate the instance, if you are an instance admin. This does not grant workspace membership. |
| **Administration** | Configure the workspace if you are a workspace admin. |
| **User settings** | Manage [your AI choice, linked accounts, notifications, sessions, and account data](./user-settings). |

Features without workspace setup do not appear in the navigation. See
[Activity](./activity) for the two activity pages. On your Practice profile, the chip beside the title
opens [the reviews that recorded something about your work](./practice-profile#reviews-of-your-work).

## Try the core workflows

### Read practice feedback

When practice reviews are on, Hephaestus reviews eligible pull requests, merge requests, and issues in connected repositories.
Feedback explains the practice, points to evidence, and suggests a next step. Feedback on the work waits for a workspace admin's approval by default. A completed review does not always produce a public comment.
Workspace admins find the workspace-wide history under **Administration → Practices → Practice reviews → Work**.
There is no separate Review activity page for members.

See [Practice feedback](./ai-code-review) for triggers, comment structure, and the personal comment-delivery setting.

### Chat with Heph

1. Open **Heph**.
2. Start a new chat.
3. Ask about a piece of feedback or recent work.

Heph can use your recent issues, pull or merge requests, and feedback as context.

See [Chat with Heph](./ai-mentor) for examples and privacy notes.

### Switch workspaces

1. Select a workspace in the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.

Each workspace has its own repositories, members, practice catalog, integrations, and settings.

## Connected services

- **GitHub or GitLab** supplies the repository activity used by the workspace.
- **Slack**, when connected, lets you chat with Heph in a direct message. Workspace admins can separately configure monitored channels.
- **Outline**, when connected, lets Hephaestus read selected team documents that are relevant to linked work. Your Outline account link identifies you as the author of your documents.

**Feedback** in the header lets you share an idea, report a bug, or send feedback to your instance administrators.
The same menu lets you open a public issue.
You can also ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hephaestus-build/Hephaestus/discussions).

# Practice feedback

Hephaestus reviews eligible work against your workspace’s practices.
It records observations before deciding whether feedback would help you.
Developers decide what to do with feedback: fix the work, ask why, or disagree.
Merge decisions stay with your reviewers.

## Read an observation

An observation says Met, Not met, Does not apply or Could not determine.
Not met lists the problems and their evidence.
Each problem has its own severity: Critical, Major, Minor or Trivial.
An open question explains what the evidence could not settle. It is never sent as feedback.

If Hephaestus cannot capture required evidence, it records no observation.
Withheld means the review stopped that candidate with a recorded reason.
It does not mean your work failed a practice.

## Where feedback appears

- On the work: a summary, line note or file note about your current change.
- On your [Practice profile](./practice-profile): private guidance about a pattern across your work.
- In conversation: private feedback that Heph can show when it fits your conversation.

On GitLab, a line note opens a discussion on the [reviewed diff lines](../contributor/practice-review-pipeline#compose-feedback).
If GitLab cannot place it on the diff, Hephaestus opens an overview discussion that links to the reviewed code.

A conversation link alone does not count as delivered feedback.
Heph must show the feedback to you.
A review can record observations without sending a comment.
Feedback about your reviewing stays on your own practice pages or in your conversation.
It does not address another author’s work as yours.

## Respond to feedback

Responses on GitHub or GitLab do not save your answer in Hephaestus.
Use the feedback link to open your own records.
Other developers see only their own records when they follow that link.

You can say whether you addressed the feedback or whether it fits your work.
Use **Disagree** if you think it is wrong, and give your reason.
You can also rate whether it helped you.
Your answer and rating stay separate, so removing a rating does not remove a disagreement.

A disagreement does not change the observation or your standing by itself.
Workspace admins can read the disagreement and inspect the observation.
Your private card text and unrelated rating notes stay private.
Undo your response to remove its current answer while preserving the history.

## Correct feedback

An admin can mark an observation incorrect when it was wrong when made.
The observation stays in history with the reason and stops supporting new feedback.
The system requests a correction to existing provider comments where the provider supports it.
An unsupported correction leaves the posted words visible with its recorded state.
Removing the mark rechecks eligibility without posting an earlier confirmed comment again.

An admin can accept an observation to remove it from open-problem lists.
Acceptance does not change the outcome or claim that you fixed the work.
It does not override an incorrect mark.

An admin can withdraw a private card whose words were wrong.
Its supporting observations stay unchanged. Restore reverses the withdrawal if the card remains eligible.
A withdrawn card accepts no new response.

## Request another review

Use **Request review** on eligible work in [Activity](./activity.mdx#request-a-review) or in the browser extension.
In **Reviews of your work** on your Practice profile, use **Request another review** in the row of a work's latest review.
A request follows your AI choice, workspace scope and source-access checks.
Once it is accepted, **View requested review** opens the new review and shows how far it got.

The review you asked from keeps its own status.
The new review keeps earlier observations in history.
The newest eligible claim is selected by recorded order rather than by who started the review.
An older review cannot silently replace newer proof.

## Stop comments

1. Open **User settings**.
2. Under **Practice feedback**, turn off **Comments on your work**.

This stops new comments on issues, pull requests, and merge requests you author, in all your workspaces.
Reviews can still record observations that workspace admins may inspect.
It does not delete earlier comments or observations.
Turn the setting on again to allow future comments.
Your AI choice is a separate account-wide limit.

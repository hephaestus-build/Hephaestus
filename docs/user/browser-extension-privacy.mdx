The Hephaestus Chrome extension has one purpose: show the practice review of the GitHub or GitLab
work you are viewing, in that work's page. You choose the Hephaestus instance that receives your
requests and each provider site where the extension may run.

The default instance is [hephaestus.build](https://hephaestus.build). Its
[Privacy notice](https://hephaestus.build/privacy) identifies the operator, legal bases, recipients,
retention and privacy contacts. If you connect to a different instance, its operator receives the
requests instead. Read that instance's Privacy notice before connecting; this page describes the
extension's behaviour across instances.

## What it reads and sends

- **Sign-in information.** Sign-in goes through your chosen instance and its identity provider.
  The extension receives an account identity and credentials for its own session. It does not read
  your GitHub or GitLab password, cookies or personal access tokens.
- **The address of the work you open.** Only after you allow a site, the extension recognises
  supported issue and pull/merge-request addresses there. As soon as you open one, it sends the
  work's canonical address to your chosen instance to look up its practice review, and again while
  the page stays open and the review may change. The address includes the provider, repository and
  work number; query strings, fragments and subpage paths are removed. It does not upload the page's
  text, source code, form inputs or screenshots, or collect a browsing history. The instance only
  resolves work in workspaces you may access.
- **A list row you choose to preview.** On a repository's or project's list of pull requests, merge
  requests or issues, the extension reads only the addresses of the rows' title links, to put a
  Hephaestus button beside each; it sends nothing. Only when you press the Hephaestus button on a row
  does it send that row's work address, in the same canonical form, to look up that one work. Other
  pages — repository pages, searches, other sites — are never looked up.
- **Review records.** Your instance returns account and workspace information and the review records
  to display: the comments it recorded posting on that work for you — where each is, which practices
  it is about, when it was delivered and the comment's address, never the comment's text — and, when
  you open the report on the work's page, your own observations on that work: each practice, its
  outcome and a one-sentence summary. The extension shows only your own records, workspace
  administrators included, and never the text of any feedback; other developers' records are in the
  Hephaestus web app, for those allowed to see them. Everything the report shows stays inside the
  extension's own frame; none of it is inserted into the provider page's document.
- **Review requests.** Asking for a review is the only change the extension makes, and it happens
  only in the extension's own confirmation window, after it re-checks the work and your access with
  the instance and you confirm there. Cancelling a review, retrying its results and approving or
  rejecting feedback happen in the Hephaestus web app.

The practice review's box, and on a list each row's Hephaestus button, are visible to the page that
hosts them, and the page can observe the box's size, which is the same in every state until you open
it. The page cannot read the review records inside it or open it. The extension does not hide that it
is installed.

## Storage and retention

The chosen instance is stored locally in your Chrome profile. Chrome also
remembers the site permissions you grant. These preferences are not synced by the extension.
Credentials stay in Chrome's session memory, restricted from page content scripts, and are removed
when Chrome closes or you sign out. Displayed account and review data are held in memory rather than
saved as a persistent extension cache.

The instance keeps its own session and security records, including authentication events; ending a
browser session is not erasure of those records. The instance's Privacy notice covers their retention
and the records already held about your work. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage
but does not erase server records or work in GitHub or GitLab.

## AI processing and other recipients

The extension does not call an AI provider. Looking up a practice review does not start an AI
review. If you request a review and confirm it, the Hephaestus server may process evidence from its connected
sources using the workspace's configured AI provider, subject to your AI choice and the workspace's
controls. Feedback may subsequently be delivered through the configured channels. A self-hosted
instance can use an external AI provider; self-hosting does not imply that all processing stays on
that server. See [Your AI choice](./privacy#your-ai-choice) and your instance's Privacy notice.

The extension contains no advertising, analytics or third-party error-reporting service. It uses
system fonts and bundled scripts and images. Opening a help or privacy link
visits the named website under that site's own privacy terms.

## Limited Use

The Hephaestus extension's use of information adheres to the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use).
Extension data is used only to provide and secure the in-page practice review described here.
It is not sold, used for advertising, used to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility,
or repurposed for unrelated profiling or research. The platform's optional academic-research
participation does not authorise collecting or reusing your extension browsing activity for research.

## Your controls and privacy requests

You can remove access to each provider site in extension settings. Signing out clears the extension's
credentials and open review views, and asks the instance to revoke that session. If the instance
cannot be reached, local sign-out still completes; revoke the session from **User settings** in the
app when it is reachable. Changing instance disconnects the old account and removes access to that
instance before you connect elsewhere. You can also revoke the extension session or sign out
everywhere from the app's **User settings**.

For access, correction, deletion or an objection to server processing, use the privacy contact in
the selected instance's Privacy notice. On the default instance, these contacts and the rights
process are in [hephaestus.build/privacy](https://hephaestus.build/privacy). Do not put tokens,
private work or personal data in a public support issue. General extension problems can be reported
through [Hephaestus support](https://github.com/hephaestus-build/Hephaestus/issues).

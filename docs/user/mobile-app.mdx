The Hephaestus app brings where you stand in your practices, your practice feedback and Heph to your phone. It works with the same
Hephaestus your team uses on the web: the same account, the same workspaces, the same feedback.
Workspace administration stays on the web.

## Sign in

1. Choose **Continue** to use hephaestus.build. If your team runs its own Hephaestus, choose **Use
   another Hephaestus** instead and enter the address you open it at in a browser, such as
   `hephaestus.example.org`.
2. Choose **Continue with GitHub** or **Continue with GitLab**. Your phone's browser opens a sign-in
   sheet; if you are already signed in to GitHub or GitLab there, you only confirm. The first time,
   this creates your Hephaestus account; after that, it signs you in. There is no separate sign-up.
3. The first time, read and accept the terms and privacy notice, as on the web. Taking part in the
   research is optional.

Your account does not come with a workspace. If you belong to one, it opens; if you belong to several,
choose one, and switch later from your account. If you belong to none yet, the app says so: your
team's Hephaestus admin adds you, or connects the GitHub organisation or GitLab group you belong to.
**Set up on the web** opens the workspace setup on the web, for admins and anyone your Hephaestus lets
create a workspace.

If the instance you choose is on your local network, iOS may ask permission to connect to it.

You stay signed in for up to seven days, or less if your team has set a shorter limit. After that,
sign in again.

## Practice

**Practice** shows where you stand in each practice group your workspace reviews, from reviews of
your own work. At the top, it counts how many of the groups with observations need attention, are mixed
or are going well, and notes how many are not observed yet; it has no scores, streaks or charts. Until
your work has been reviewed, it says so and points you to what each practice group looks for.

- **Suggested next step**: when a group has one, the next step Hephaestus suggests for the group that
  most needs your attention, quoted as it is written.
- **Practice groups**: each group with observations, most in need of attention first: **Needs attention**,
  **Mixed feedback**, **Going well** or **Nothing to report yet**, which way it has been moving when
  there is enough reviewed work to say, how its practices stand, and the work it is based on.
- **Not observed yet**: groups no reviewed work has touched yet. Open one to see what its practices
  look for.

Open a group to see where you stand and what that rests on, its suggested next step, each of its
practices, its direction and its most recent reviews. **Direction** says whether recent reviewed work
carried more strengths or more problems than the stretch before it, or that there is not enough
relevant reviewed work yet to compare, and how much reviewed work it rests on.

Open a practice for your next step, every observation behind your standing (**To work on** and
**Going well**), why the practice matters and what good looks like. Open an observation to see what the
review found and why, the exact lines it quoted from your work and where they are, and a link to the
work. If the practice has changed since the review, or the version of the practice it was reviewed against is not
known, the observation says so. **All review history** lists every review in a group or practice,
newest first.

When you open an observation from the review history, you can answer the feedback it was delivered
as, the same as on the feedback's own page.

## Practice feedback

**Practice feedback**, in Practice, lists the feedback written about your reviewed work, with a short
note from Heph, your AI mentor, at the top. The note only says how much feedback is there and offers to
talk it through; the feedback itself is shown unchanged. If Heph is not available to you, the list shows
the feedback without the note. If the list cannot refresh, it keeps what it loaded earlier and says
so. Open a piece of feedback to see where it was seen, why the practice matters, and where you stand in
that practice.

Opening your feedback is what delivers it, the same as on the web. The app only reads it while
Practice feedback is on screen, so nothing counts as delivered while your phone is in your pocket, and
the rest of Practice never shows how much feedback there is.

Under **Your answer**, tell Hephaestus whether the feedback was **Helpful** or **Not helpful**, and
what you did with it: **Addressed**, **Disputed** or **Not applicable**. When you dispute feedback,
say why. You can change your answer or take it back later.

Under the feedback, **Talk it through** opens a new conversation with Heph with a question about that
feedback already written for you. Edit it as you like; nothing is sent until you send it.

## Heph

**Heph**, the second tab, lists your conversations, newest first, and suggests a few questions to start from; before
your first conversation, Heph introduces itself there. Start a new conversation with the compose
button at the top. Heph works the same as on the web, and your conversations appear in both places.
See [Chat with Heph](./ai-mentor).

Before the first message you send in a conversation, the app asks whether to share it with the AI
service Heph answers with. That service is chosen by the people who run your Hephaestus; their privacy
notice names it. If you cancel, your message stays in the box and nothing is sent. The app asks again
for each conversation, and again after the app restarts or you sign in again.

If a reply is offensive, harmful or wrong, choose **Report** under it. You can say why; the report goes
to the people who run your Hephaestus with that reply and your reason, not the rest of the
conversation. Practice feedback has **Report this feedback** at the bottom of its page.

When you send a message, the conversation moves to it, and Heph's answer appears below it and stays in
view as it grows. If you scroll up to read something earlier while Heph is answering, the conversation
stays where you are; **Jump to latest**, or scrolling back to the end, takes you back and follows the
answer again.

If an answer stops, cannot finish, or ends early, the conversation says so under it and offers to
send your question again. It is sent as a new message, so both stay in the conversation. If you leave the app while Heph is answering, the answer stops. When you come back, the conversation
shows where it stopped and offers to try again.

If Heph is not available to you, the tab says so. Ask your workspace administrator for access.

## Your account

Your GitHub or GitLab picture, at the top right of each tab, opens your account. The close button (✕) at its top right closes it from any page in it; your settings take effect as you change them, so there is nothing to save.

- **Workspace**: which workspace is open; if you belong to several, switch to another.
- **Notifications**: turn push notifications on or off. The app never asks until you choose to turn
  them on here. Your choice holds for this sign-in: after you sign out and in again, turn them on again
  if you want them. A notification only says that new practice feedback is waiting, never what it says,
  because it can appear on your lock screen. Some teams' Hephaestus does not send notifications; the
  app tells you when that is the case, and new feedback still appears under Practice feedback.
- **Signed-in devices**: see where you are signed in, on your phone and in browsers, and sign any of them out.
- **Privacy and account**: read the privacy notice, change your research answer, export your data,
  or delete your account. You can reach it before accepting the terms too, from the link at the bottom
  of the terms, and without a workspace, so you can delete an account you do not want to keep.
- **About Hephaestus**: the app's version, which Hephaestus you are connected to, and where to read
  more or report a problem.
- **Sign out**: signs this phone out. If your phone is offline, it is signed out right away, and
  the app ends the session on the server the next time you open it with a connection.

## Offline

While the app stays open, what you last loaded stays readable when you lose your connection, with a
note that it could not be refreshed. The app does not keep feedback or conversations on your phone
after it closes, so opening it again needs a connection. Answers, conversations and settings need one
too.

## Accessibility

The app follows your phone's text size, dark mode and screen reader. See also
[Accessibility](./accessibility).

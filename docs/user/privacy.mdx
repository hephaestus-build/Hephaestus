Hephaestus reads connected project work to give you practice feedback and help you talk it through with Heph.
**Your work can be processed even if you have never signed in.**

Open **Privacy** in the app's footer for your instance's responsible organisation, privacy contact, legal
bases, providers, and data locations. This guide explains the product; that notice covers your deployment.
If the notice or contact is missing, ask the organisation running your instance.

## Signing in and choosing research participation

The sign-in screen links to your instance's privacy notice and imprint, so you can read both before
choosing a provider. Setup is then one page with two separate decisions, both visible before you
commit to either.

The first is **Terms and privacy**: three short points — what Hephaestus reads, that its feedback is
written by an AI model and can be wrong, and where the privacy notice for your instance is — and a box
to accept the terms. The terms ask two things of you: keep to the work you are entitled to see, and
treat feedback as guidance for the person it is addressed to rather than an assessment to pass on.
Accepting them is not consent to research.

The second appears only if the organisation running your instance also runs a study, and it names that
organisation on the screen. It asks: **Yes, take part**, or **No, don't take part** for the same
service without opting in. Neither answer is selected for you and both cost the same click. Where no
study is configured the question is not asked and the terms alone complete setup.

**Continue** records your decisions together, against the version of the wording you were shown. You
can change the research answer later; see
[Your choices and right to object](#your-choices-and-right-to-object).

## What Hephaestus reads

Workspace admins choose the connected sources and what runs in the workspace:

- **GitHub and GitLab:** contributor profiles, repository and team membership, issues, pull requests or merge
  requests, reviews, comments, discussions, and commit metadata. Practice reviews can also read relevant code
  changes, repository snapshots and reachable Git history.
- **Slack:** identities, privacy choices, your direct conversations with Heph, and new messages in monitored
  channels. Channel monitoring requires separate activation, a visible announcement, and participant privacy
  controls; connecting Slack alone does not activate it.
- **Outline:** selected collections and documents, their content, and author information.

Practice reviews can read all permitted workspace areas and repositories available to the review,
including Slack threads and person-scoped observation and feedback history. There is no fixed
record-count or history-window cap for that context. Privacy choices, access boundaries and erasure
checks still apply. Generated-path settings mark retained evidence; they do not remove it. Refusing
a bot's work for review does not erase its source records.

These sources provide context for practice reviews and mentoring. A connection does not authorise every use
of its content. Hephaestus records **observations and practice feedback**, including observations that never
become comments. [Activity](./activity) counts and lists the pull requests, reviews, issues and comments
you do in the workspace's repositories, and your open work, such as review requests and assigned
issues. It lists members by name and never ranks them.

Your sign-in details, linked accounts, memberships, preferences, chats, and feedback reactions support your
account and conversations. Product-feedback submissions (with the page path and browser identification you choose to attach, and the
Hephaestus release that received them), survey invitations, answers, and declines support product improvement.
A survey labelled **Research** is offered only while you take part in the research programme your instance names;
its answers are that study's data, collected under your research consent.
Authentication events (including IP address and browser information), settings-change history, review
diagnostics, and AI usage accounting support security, troubleshooting, and spending controls.

The app uses essential cookies and browser storage for sign-in, security, and preferences. Optional browser **Sentry
error monitoring** sends error reports only if the operator configures it and you opt in. Server error
monitoring is a separate operator setting; browser consent does not control it. Your instance must
disclose its diagnostic recipients, basis and retention.

When a page displays an avatar from a source platform, your browser requests that image from its
host. That host receives network metadata such as your IP address and the requested image address,
including on public pages without sign-in. Its retention is separate from Hephaestus.

Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal or sensitive information in chats, source content, or feedback.

## Your AI choice

[Your AI choice](./workspace#your-ai-choice) sets which models may run practice reviews about you
and handle your Heph requests. **In-house** allows models your organisation says it operates. **Cloud** also
allows models operated by providers your organisation approved. A provider may process, keep, or
review what it receives under its own agreement with your organisation; the choice does not set a
retention period or a training policy. **No AI** stops new practice reviews about you, new requests to Heph and new feedback delivery. It does not stop source synchronisation, stored work, access by authorised people,
or requests already sent to a provider. Your work can still be part of shared repository context in
reviews about other developers; your choice does not remove that content or change their choices or model routes.
Person-scoped observation and feedback history is checked separately against your AI choice. You answer once, and your answer applies in
all your workspaces on this deployment. Change it from **Your AI choice** in any workspace's sidebar
or in **User settings**. Anything stricter than your answer also counts, nothing switches you to
anything looser, and a workspace adding models never asks you again. This choice is distinct from
research participation and from linking an account. A workspace owner declares who operates each
model. Ask them about the actual provider, location, retention, and training terms. Slack Heph uses the same choice. Its App Home explains when Heph is off for you,
requires a choice, or has no eligible model. Channel-message controls remain available when Heph is
unavailable. A failed Home refresh can leave an older status visible; a new interaction still checks
eligibility.

## AI processing and visibility

Enabled AI features send relevant code, discussion, documents, conversation history, and observation evidence
to the workspace's configured AI provider. **Self-hosting does not necessarily keep AI requests local.** Ask
your workspace admin which provider is configured; your instance's Privacy notice covers recipients,
international transfers, retention, and training terms.

Practice feedback is advisory; it does not grade you or replace human review.

- Members can read [Activity](./activity). It does not check each viewer's permissions on the source
  platform. Your Practice profile, its feedback and your Heph conversations are private through
  normal member access. Workspace admins can read observations, delivery metadata and dispute
  explanations, but not private practice-page or conversation feedback text. Ratings and other
  private response notes are not shared with them.
- A publicly viewable workspace shows its members, teams, repositories and practices to anyone, without
  signing in. Activity, practice pages, feedback and Heph conversations are not public. Your instance's
  contributor list is also public.
- Feedback posted on GitHub, GitLab, or Slack is visible to that destination's audience.
- Instance administrators can use **View as user** to read your activity,
  practice pages, feedback, and existing Heph conversations, even before your first Hephaestus
  sign-in. Each read is recorded with a stated reason; it cannot send messages, answer questions,
  or make choices for you, and it does not count as you having seen your feedback. Workspace
  administrators do not have this access.
- The people who run your instance can access stored data for operation and privacy requests.
  Product-feedback and survey submissions are available to instance administrators.

## Your choices and right to object

- **Comments and Slack reminders:** in **User settings → Practice feedback**, turn this off to stop new
  comments on your work and related Slack reminders. **Reviews still run and admins can still see
  observations.** Turning it back on allows new deliveries; neither action removes existing comments.
- **Slack channel messages:** in the Hephaestus App Home, select **Stop using my messages** to stop collection
  and erase your collected channel messages and related conversation observations and feedback. **Allow
  future messages** permits new collection without restoring deleted data. This does not erase mentor DMs,
  unrelated repository feedback, Slack's copy, or [separately retained copies](#copies-outside-active-records).
- **Academic research participation:** where offered, use **User settings → Academic research participation →
  Participate in academic research** to record or withdraw your agreement. If you use Hephaestus in
  Slack, its App Home links to these account settings.
  Withdrawal does not stop ordinary practice reviews or erase stored data, and it ends research survey
  invitations. Contact your instance's privacy contact about data already shared for research, including
  research survey answers, and withdrawal from a specific study.
- **Email notifications:** where offered, optional invitations, connection alerts and administrator
  updates start off. Choose each category in **User settings → Email notifications**, or use the
  unsubscribe link in an email without signing in. Research invitation email also requires research
  participation; switching off the emails does not withdraw that participation. The mail relay receives
  your contact address, notification type and links, not product-feedback text or survey answers.
  Account-security and deletion notices are separate from these optional subscriptions.
- **Optional error reports:** open **Cookie preferences** in the app footer, or **User settings → Privacy →
  Change cookie choices**, to grant or withdraw consent where this option is offered.

### Object to processing

Contact your instance's privacy contact to object to processing or request access, correction, restriction,
or erasure as applicable. Turning off comments, leaving a workspace, or never signing in does not stop
processing. Your instance's notice also explains how to complain to a supervisory authority.

## Correct or dispute feedback

Follow **Answer or dispute it in Hephaestus** on a GitHub or GitLab comment. The page shows your own
reviews of that work. Choose **Disputed** and give a reason; workspace admins read that explanation.
A standing dispute holds back the same point in later reviews of the same work, except for a committed
secret. Withdrawing your dispute allows that point again. Reactions or replies on GitHub or GitLab
do not enter this dispute process.

On your Practice profile, **Not accurate** also records a dispute. An admin can mark an observation
incorrect or withdraw practice-page feedback with a reason and later restore it. Unseen withdrawn
feedback does not appear; feedback you had seen becomes a withdrawal notice. The original and its
history remain stored, but its text leaves future review and Heph context. Already-sent replies,
inline provider comments and context already handed to a running review are not recalled.
Disputing or withdrawing feedback is not erasure. Ask the privacy contact for wider correction,
restriction or erasure, including if you cannot use the app.

## Get a copy of your data

Open **User settings → Danger Zone → Export my data → Request export**. When ready, select **Download**.
If generation fails, use **Retry export**; contact the operator for repeated failures or stalls. Downloads
expire **48 hours** after preparation.

The JSON file contains your account, linked sign-in identities, memberships, account features, preferences,
your AI choice, and authentication history—not credentials or session secrets. Keep it private.

**The self-service export is limited.** Request conversations, observations and feedback (including
never-delivered results), activity records, mirrored source content, product-feedback submissions, survey
responses, and other retained copies through your instance's privacy contact. An instance administrator
can resolve your exact provider identities, preview the scope and download one JSON file for the wider
request. It includes selected locally mirrored source content, but excludes credentials, other
people's profiles, raw Git objects and hidden runtime journals. Access and portability are different
rights: statutory portability depends on the applicable consent/contract basis and other conditions.
You do not need a Hephaestus account for this procedure. Obtain original
source-platform exports from those platforms separately.

## Delete your account or request wider erasure

Open **User settings → Danger Zone → Delete account**, select **Delete**, and follow the confirmation prompt.
You are signed out on all devices immediately. If your instance sends email and your account has a
verified address, a confirmation naming the cleanup date is sent to that address. After the default **48-hour cooldown**, scheduled cleanup
removes your sign-in details, linked identities, AI choice, product-feedback submissions, and survey responses, leaving
an account record with its contact details cleared. There is no self-service undo; contact the operator
promptly if the request was a mistake.

**Account deletion is not complete person erasure.** Contributor profiles, conversations, observations,
feedback, and other retained copies need a wider request. Disconnecting a source or removing a workspace
is not a substitute, and deletion in Hephaestus does not delete your original work on connected services.

Whether or not you have an account, send the instance's privacy contact your workspace, relevant provider
instance and native user ID (for Slack, also its workspace ID), and links to affected work or feedback.
If you do not know the native IDs, send source-profile and work links so the operator can resolve
them from the provider. You do not need to find database IDs or sign in to submit a request.
The operator must verify which records belong to you. Names, logins and email addresses are not used
as matching keys. Do not send passwords, tokens, or unnecessary identity documents.

Include already-posted feedback explicitly: correction or removal requires a separate, best-effort operator
request. The operator previews exact rows and provider locations, then runs an erasure job with a completion
record. Changed scope requires a new preview. The job stops affected reviews, waits for worker evidence
removal, and clears affected hidden Heph memory. A failed step or offline worker requires a resume;
it is not completed erasure. Minimal exact native identity keys remain to prevent renewed product
processing; source sync may still mirror upstream records. Ask for the request's scope, status, and
any retention or lawful exceptions. The operator normally answers within one month; a justified
extension of up to two further months requires notice within the first month.

## How long data stays

Every category has its own window and an operator can change several of them, so the exact durations are
stated once, in the [record of processing](/admin/dsms/record-of-processing): it lists each shipped window
and which of them an operator controls. Your instance's Privacy notice states what your deployment
applies. Scheduled removal follows eligibility and may require later successful runs to finish. Account
and export timing is described above.

### Project content

- **GitHub and GitLab:** active copies stay while a workspace monitors the source. Removing the last relevant
  connection or repository monitor removes them. Shared sources remain for other monitoring workspaces;
  contributor profiles can remain independently and need operator-assisted erasure. A repository that
  is unavailable or loses access keeps its stored work while it is monitored. Removing a note on
  GitLab changes live context only after successful reconciliation; historical review copies do not
  change automatically.
- **Slack channel threads:** kept for a rolling window measured from the thread's latest message, so an
  active thread can still contain much older messages. This window does not apply to mentor DMs.
- **Outline:** selected content stays while its collection and connection remain active.
- **Web chats, Slack mentor DMs, and memberships:** retained with their workspace records;
  removed through workspace removal or verified person erasure.
- **League points and XP from the retired leaderboard:** deleted by the upgrade that retired the
  leaderboard. The operator may have exported a copy before upgrading; that copy is kept outside
  Hephaestus, for as long as the operator decides.
- **Product-feedback and survey submissions:** no automatic time-based expiry; removed with account deletion,
  with their workspace where applicable, or through verified erasure.

### Security and review history

- **Authentication history:** expires in monthly batches. Account erasure redacts identifying
  authentication details.
- **Settings-change history:** expires on its own window. Account erasure detaches actor references, not
  the change record.
- **Review diagnostics:** become eligible for removal once the job has been finished for long enough. Job
  history becomes eligible later, and only when observations and feedback no longer reference it. Pending
  delivery delays both. The job-retention service must also be enabled; switching off reviews does not
  automatically delete their old records.
- **Review working copies:** worker attempt folders are removed after evidence admission; ended attempts
  have a one-hour cleanup grace, and restart cleanup removes abandoned folders. Failed cleanup needs
  a later successful run. Raw Git mirrors follow repository monitoring; the repository owner must
  handle Git authorship at source.
- **AI usage accounting:** contains token counts, costs, and run identifiers, not message content, and is
  kept long enough for annual accounting; an operator can extend that for a legal obligation.
- **Other operational and administration history:** some records of who configured or requested an operation
  have no automatic expiry and survive account or workspace removal. Include them in a verified erasure request.

### Copies outside active records

- **Buffered integration events:** expire within hours for Slack and Outline, and within months for GitHub
  and GitLab, under the shipped limits. Storage limits can shorten retention; individual erasure cannot
  selectively remove these copies.
- **Logs, support bundles, and backups:** container logs rotate by size, not age. The operator controls
  support-bundle and backup retention. These copies do not support immediate selective erasure; ask about
  expiry and how erased data is handled after a restore.
- **AI-provider copies and posted feedback:** governed by the provider's policies. Completed AI requests
  cannot be retracted; posted feedback follows the separate removal process above.

The person-data upgrade resets hidden Heph runtime memory once because legacy copies cannot be
attributed safely. Your visible conversations, titles and times remain, but you may need to repeat
context. This is separate from an erasure request.

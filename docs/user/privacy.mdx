Hephaestus reads connected project work to give you practice feedback and help you discuss it with Heph.
**Hephaestus can process your work even if you have never signed in.**

**Privacy** in the app footer gives your instance’s responsible organization, privacy contact, legal bases, providers, and data locations.
This page explains how the product behaves and what you control.
Your instance’s notice gives the legal and provider details for your deployment.

For a specific task:

- [Change your AI choice](#your-ai-choice).
- [Stop comments or control Slack messages](#your-choices-and-right-to-object).
- [Get a copy of your data](#get-a-copy-of-your-data).
- [Delete your account or request wider erasure](#delete-your-account-or-request-wider-erasure).

1. Open **Privacy** in the app footer.
2. If the notice or contact is missing, ask the organization that runs your instance.

## Signing in and choosing research participation

The sign-in screen links to your instance’s privacy notice and imprint.
You can read both before you choose a provider.
Setup then shows one page with two separate decisions.
You see both before you make either.

The first, **Terms and privacy**, has three short points:

- What Hephaestus reads.
- That an AI model writes its feedback and can be wrong.
- Where to find your instance’s privacy notice.

To accept the terms, select **I accept the terms of use**.
The terms require you to access only work you have permission to see.
They also require you to treat feedback as guidance for its recipient, not an assessment to share.
Acceptance is not research consent.

The second decision appears only if the organization that runs your instance also runs a study.
The screen names that organization.
It offers **Yes, take part** or **`No, don't take part`**, with the same service if you do not participate.
Neither answer starts selected. Both need one selection.
Without a configured study, the terms alone complete setup.

**Continue** records both decisions against the version of the text you saw.
You can change your research answer later.
[Your choices and right to object](#your-choices-and-right-to-object) explains how.

## What Hephaestus reads

Workspace admins choose the connected sources and what runs in the workspace:

- **GitHub and GitLab:** contributor profiles, repository and team membership, and commit metadata. They also supply issues, pull requests or merge requests, reviews, comments, and discussions. Practice reviews can also read relevant code changes, repository snapshots, and reachable Git history.
- **Slack:** identities, privacy choices, direct conversations with Heph, and new messages in monitored channels. Channel monitoring requires separate activation, a visible announcement, and participant privacy controls. A Slack connection alone does not activate it. Each person in a monitored thread gets a separate review. That review judges only that person’s own messages.
- **Outline:** selected collections and documents, their content, and author information.

Practice reviews can read all permitted workspace areas and repositories available to the review.
This includes Slack threads and observation and feedback history scoped to a person.
There is no fixed limit on the number of records or the history period for that context.
Privacy choices, access boundaries, and erasure checks still apply.

Generated-path settings mark retained evidence but do not remove it.
Refusal to review a bot’s work does not erase its source records.
A connection alone does not authorize all uses of its content.
These sources supply context for practice reviews and the mentor.

Hephaestus records **observations and practice feedback**, including observations that never become comments.
[Activity](./activity) counts and lists your pull requests, reviews, issues, and comments in workspace repositories.
It also lists your open work, such as review requests and assigned issues.
It lists members by name and never ranks them.

Your sign-in details, connected accounts, memberships, preferences, chats, and feedback reactions support your account and conversations.
Product-feedback submissions support product improvement, as do survey invitations, answers, and declines.
Submissions include the page path and browser identification you choose to attach, plus the Hephaestus release that received them.
A **Research** survey is available only while you participate in the research program your instance names.
Its answers are study data collected under your research consent.

These records support security, troubleshooting, and spending controls:

- Authentication events, including IP address and browser information.
- History of changes to settings.
- Review diagnostics.
- AI usage accounting.

The app uses essential cookies and browser storage for sign-in, security, and preferences.
Optional browser **Sentry error monitoring** sends reports only if the operator configures it and you opt in.
Server error monitoring is a separate operator setting.
Browser consent does not control it.
Your instance must disclose diagnostic recipients, basis, and retention.

When a page shows an avatar from a source platform, your browser requests the image from its host.
That host receives network metadata, such as your IP address and the image address.
This also occurs on public pages without sign-in.
The host’s retention is separate from Hephaestus.

1. Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal or sensitive information in chats, source content, or feedback.

## Your AI choice

[Your AI choice](./workspace#your-ai-choice) sets which models may review practices about you and handle your Heph requests.
**In-house** allows models your organization says it operates.
**Cloud** also allows models operated by providers your organization approved.
A provider may process, keep, or review received data under its agreement with your organization.
Your choice does not set a retention period or training policy.

**No AI** stops new practice reviews about you, new Heph requests, and new feedback delivery.
It does not stop source sync, stored work, access by authorized people, or requests already sent to a provider.
Your work can still appear in shared repository context for reviews about other developers.
Your choice does not remove that content or change their choices or model routes.
Hephaestus checks observation and feedback history scoped to you separately against your AI choice.

You answer once, across all your workspaces on this deployment.
**Cloud** allows In-house models too.
**In-house** does not allow Cloud models.
Hephaestus never changes your answer to allow more AI processing.
New workspace models do not require another answer.
This choice is separate from research participation and connected accounts.

1. To change your answer, open **Your AI choice** in a workspace sidebar or **User settings**.
2. Ask the workspace owner about the actual provider, location, retention, and training terms.

The owner declares who operates each model.
Slack Heph uses the same choice.
Its App Home explains when Heph is off for you, requires a choice, or has no eligible model.
Channel-message controls stay available when Heph is unavailable.
A failed Home refresh can leave an older status visible.
Each new interaction still checks eligibility.

## AI processing and visibility

Active AI features send relevant code, discussion, documents, conversation history, and observation evidence to the workspace’s configured AI provider.
**Self-hosting does not necessarily keep AI requests local.**
Your instance’s privacy notice covers recipients, international transfers, retention, and training terms.

1. Ask your workspace admin which provider is configured.

Practice feedback is guidance, not a grade or a replacement for human review.
Access differs by role and destination:

- Members can read [Activity](./activity) without individual source-platform permission checks. Normal member access keeps your Practice profile, its feedback, and Heph conversations private. Workspace admins can read observations, delivery metadata, and dispute explanations. They cannot read private practice-page or conversation feedback text, ratings, or other private response notes.
- A publicly viewable workspace shows members, teams, repositories, and practices to anyone without sign-in. Activity, practice pages, feedback, and Heph conversations are not public. Your instance’s contributor list is also public.
- Feedback on GitHub, GitLab, or Slack is visible to that destination’s audience.
- Instance admins can use **View as user** to read activity, practice pages, feedback, and existing Heph conversations. This includes access before your first Hephaestus sign-in. Each read has a recorded reason. This access cannot send messages, answer questions, or make choices for you. It does not count as you seeing feedback. Workspace admins do not have this access.
- Instance operators can access stored data for operation and privacy requests. Instance admins can read product-feedback and survey submissions.

## Your choices and right to object

### Comments and Slack reminders

1. Open **User settings → Practice feedback**.
2. Turn off **Comments and Slack reminders** to stop new comments on your work and related Slack reminders.
3. To allow new deliveries again, turn it on.

**Reviews still run and admins can still see observations.**
Neither action removes existing comments.

### Slack channel messages

1. In the Hephaestus App Home, select **Stop using my messages**.
2. To permit new collection, select **Allow future messages**.

The first action stops collection and erases collected channel messages and related conversation observations and feedback.
The second does not restore deleted data.
Neither erases mentor DMs, unrelated repository feedback, Slack’s copy, or [separately retained copies](#copies-outside-active-records).

### Academic research participation

1. Where offered, open **User settings → Academic research participation → Participate in academic research**.
2. Turn it on to record your agreement, or off to withdraw it.
3. For data already shared for research or withdrawal from a specific study, contact your instance’s privacy contact.

Slack App Home links to these account settings.
Withdrawal ends research survey invitations but does not stop ordinary practice reviews or erase stored data.
Existing research data includes research survey answers.

### Email notifications

Optional invitations, connection alerts, and admin updates start off where offered.

1. Choose each category in **User settings → Email notifications**.
2. To unsubscribe without sign-in, use the unsubscribe link in an email.

Research invitation emails also require research participation.
Disabling emails does not withdraw participation.
The mail relay receives your contact address, notification type, and links, not product-feedback text or survey answers.
Account-security and deletion notices are separate from optional subscriptions.

### Optional error reports

1. Open **Cookie preferences** in the app footer, or **User settings → Privacy → Change cookie choices**.
2. Where offered, select **Allow error reports** to grant consent or **Decline error reports** to withdraw it.

### Object to processing

1. Contact your instance’s privacy contact to object or request access, correction, restriction, or erasure, as applicable.

Disabling comments, leaving a workspace, or never signing in does not stop processing.
Your instance’s notice also explains how to complain to a supervisory authority.

## Correct or dispute feedback

1. Follow **Answer or dispute it in Hephaestus** on a GitHub or GitLab comment.
2. Select **Disputed**.
3. Give a reason for workspace admins to read.

The page shows your own reviews of that work.
A standing dispute withholds feedback about the same claim in later reviews of the same work.
**Not applicable** also withholds feedback about that claim.
Withdrawal of the dispute allows that point again.
Reactions or replies on GitHub or GitLab do not enter this process.

On your Practice profile, **Not accurate** also records a dispute.
An admin can mark an observation incorrect or withdraw practice-page feedback with a reason, then restore it later.
Unseen withdrawn feedback does not appear.
Feedback you saw becomes a withdrawal notice.
The original and its history stay stored, but its text leaves future review and Heph context.

Hephaestus cannot recall replies already sent, inline provider comments, or context already given to a review that still runs.
A dispute or withdrawal is not erasure.

1. For wider correction, restriction, or erasure, ask the privacy contact, including if you cannot use the app.

## Get a copy of your data

1. Open **User settings → Danger zone → Export your data → Request export**.
2. If the export fails, select **Request export again**.
3. For repeated failures or stalled exports, contact the operator.
4. When the export is ready, select **Download**.
5. Keep the downloaded file private.

Downloads expire **48 hours** after preparation.
The JSON file includes your account, connected sign-in identities, memberships, account features, preferences, AI choice, and authentication history.
It does not include credentials or session secrets.

**The self-service export is limited.**
The privacy contact handles requests for these additional records:

- Conversations, observations, and feedback, including results never delivered.
- Activity records and mirrored source content.
- Product-feedback submissions and survey responses.
- Other retained copies.

An instance admin can resolve your exact provider identities, preview the scope, and download one JSON file for the wider request.
It includes selected source content mirrored locally.
It excludes credentials, other people’s profiles, raw Git objects, and hidden runtime journals.
Access and portability are different rights.
Statutory portability depends on the applicable consent or contract basis and other conditions.

1. Request additional records through your instance’s privacy contact.
2. Obtain original source-platform exports from those platforms separately.

You do not need a Hephaestus account for this procedure.

## Delete your account or request wider erasure

**CAUTION:** Account deletion disables your account immediately and leads to permanent cleanup after the cooldown.
There is no self-service undo.
Do not delete your account to leave one workspace or sign out a device.

1. Open **User settings → Danger zone**.
2. Select **Delete account**.
3. In the dialog, enter the confirmation phrase.
4. Select **Delete account** again.
5. If the request was a mistake, contact the operator immediately.

Hephaestus immediately signs you out on all devices.
If your instance sends email and your account has a verified address, it sends confirmation with the cleanup date.
After the default **48-hour cooldown**, scheduled cleanup removes sign-in details, connected identities, AI choice, product-feedback submissions, and survey responses.
An account record remains with contact details cleared.
There is no self-service undo.

**Account deletion is not complete person erasure.**
Contributor profiles, conversations, observations, feedback, and other retained copies require a wider request.
A source disconnection or workspace removal is not a substitute.
Deletion in Hephaestus does not delete original work on connected services.

With or without an account:

1. Send the instance’s privacy contact your workspace, relevant provider instance, native user ID, and links to affected work or feedback.
2. For Slack, also include its workspace ID.
3. If native IDs are unknown, send source-profile and work links for the operator to resolve them.
4. Do not send passwords, tokens, or unnecessary identity documents.
5. Include already-posted feedback explicitly.
6. Ask for the request’s scope, status, and any retention or lawful exceptions.

You do not need database IDs or sign-in to submit a request.
The operator must verify which records belong to you.
Names, logins, and email addresses are not matching keys.
Correction or removal of posted feedback requires a separate operator request, with no guarantee of success.

The operator confirms the scope before erasure.
Hephaestus records completion.
Erasure stops affected reviews and removes their working evidence and affected hidden Heph memory.
If a step fails or a worker is offline, the request stays incomplete until the operator resumes it.

Minimal exact native identity keys stay to prevent renewed product processing.
Source sync may still mirror upstream records.
The operator normally answers within one month.
A justified extension of up to two more months requires notice within the first month.

## How long data stays

Each category has its own retention period.
An operator can change several periods.
The [record of processing](/admin/dsms/record-of-processing) lists each period supplied with Hephaestus and identifies those the operator controls.
Your instance’s privacy notice states which periods your deployment applies.

Scheduled removal starts after records become eligible and may need later successful runs to finish.
The sections above describe account and export timing.

### Project content

- **GitHub and GitLab:** active copies stay while a workspace monitors the source. Removal of the last relevant connection or repository monitor removes them. Shared sources stay for other workspaces that monitor them. Contributor profiles can stay independently and require operator-assisted erasure.

  An unavailable repository, or one that loses access, keeps stored work while monitored. A removed GitLab note leaves live context only after successful reconciliation. Historical review copies do not change automatically.

- **Slack channel threads:** retention uses a rolling period measured from the latest message. An active thread can still contain much older messages. This period does not apply to mentor DMs.
- **Outline:** selected content stays while its collection and connection stay active.
- **Web chats, Slack mentor DMs, and memberships:** records stay with the workspace. Workspace removal or verified person erasure removes them.
- **League points and XP from the retired leaderboard:** the upgrade that retired the leaderboard deleted them. The operator may have exported a copy before the upgrade. That copy stays outside Hephaestus for as long as the operator decides.
- **Product-feedback and survey submissions:** there is no automatic expiry by age. Account deletion, applicable workspace removal, or verified erasure removes them.

### Security and review history

- **Authentication history:** records expire in monthly batches. Account erasure redacts identifying authentication details.
- **Settings-change history:** records expire after their own retention period. Account erasure detaches actor references, not the change record.
- **Review diagnostics:** records become eligible for removal when enough time has passed since job completion. Job history becomes eligible later, only without observation or feedback references. Pending delivery delays both. The job-retention service must also be on. Disabling reviews does not automatically delete old records.
- **Review working copies:** cleanup removes worker attempt folders after evidence admission. Ended attempts have a one-hour cleanup grace period. Restart cleanup removes abandoned folders. Failed cleanup needs a later successful run. Raw Git mirrors follow repository monitoring. The repository owner must handle Git authorship at source.
- **AI usage accounting:** records contain token counts, costs, and run identifiers, not message content. They stay long enough for annual accounting. An operator can extend retention for a legal obligation.
- **Other operational and administration history:** some records of who configured or requested an operation have no automatic expiry. They survive account or workspace removal. A verified erasure request must include them.

### Copies outside active records

- **Buffered integration events:** supplied limits expire Slack and Outline events within hours, and GitHub and GitLab events within months. Storage limits can shorten retention. Individual erasure cannot selectively remove these copies.
- **Logs, support bundles, and backups:** container logs rotate by size, not age. The operator controls support-bundle and backup retention. These copies do not support immediate selective erasure. The operator can explain expiry and treatment of erased data after a restore.
- **AI-provider copies and posted feedback:** provider policies apply. Completed AI requests cannot be recalled. Posted feedback follows the separate removal process above.

The upgrade that introduced person-data controls reset hidden Heph memory once.
Visible conversations, titles, and times stayed, but you may need to repeat context from before that upgrade.
This historical reset is separate from an erasure request.

Open **User settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. These settings belong to your account;
workspace administration is separate.

## Your AI choice

A workspace can ask you to set up on your first visit, after your membership is already in place. The
setup page is one page in two parts: which AI may handle your work, and the Slack or Outline accounts
your workspace asks you to connect. It does not approve or create membership.

You answer the AI question once, and your answer applies in all your workspaces on this Hephaestus
deployment. Joining another workspace never asks it again; that workspace's setup page appears only if
it requires an account you have not connected. Your answer is a boundary, not a pick from a list of
models: anything stricter than your answer also counts, and nothing ever switches you to anything
looser.

The cards compare the three answers on the same four rows:

| | **In-house** | **Cloud** | **No AI** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AI help** | Feedback and Heph | Feedback and Heph | No new feedback or Heph replies |
| **Models** | Only models your organisation runs, usually smaller ones | In-house models plus cloud models, usually the strongest on offer | None |
| **Speed** | Limited by your organisation's capacity, so replies can be slower | More capacity, usually faster | Not applicable |
| **Sent to** | Only systems your organisation runs | Also a cloud provider your organisation configured; its agreement sets retention and access | Nowhere; sync and stored work continue |

Each workspace sets up its own models within these answers. When nothing in the workspace you are
looking at is set up within an answer, its card says so: that answer is *not set up here yet*,
nothing runs for you for that purpose in this workspace, and Hephaestus never switches you to
another answer. Wait for a workspace owner to add a model, or change your answer.
"Set up" describes the configuration, not current service health or a guarantee of speed. In-house
models depend on your organisation's capacity and storage rules, and allowing the cloud does not
guarantee better models or faster replies. A model mark names its maker, not who runs it. For
example, an OpenAI model served by Azure is not automatically in-house because its endpoint is
private. The Logos mark names the Logos service, not who runs it; an organisation can run Logos
itself or use a provider-operated Logos deployment. Ask a workspace owner who operates the
deployment and what terms apply to your work.

In a workspace, each answer's card shows the model a new request reaches first, with its maker's
logo and the service that receives its requests. If more models are ready, the card shows the count,
and the list below the cards names every model for your selected answer with its declared handling.
In user settings, the cards show no model or service: your answer applies to every workspace, and
each workspace can use a different service.

On your first visit, nothing is preselected. Choose an answer and select **Continue**; on a return
visit the button reads **Save**. You can choose any answer, including one no workspace has set up yet.
A workspace adding, removing or re-declaring models never asks you again: your answer stays in force
and is checked on every request.

To change your answer at any time, open **Your AI choice** in the sidebar of any workspace, which also
shows that workspace's account links and whether anything there runs within your answer, or open
**Your AI choice** in **User settings**. Existing feedback and earlier conversations with Heph remain
readable. With **No AI**, there are no new practice reviews about you, new requests to Heph, or new
feedback delivery. Requests already sent cannot be recalled. You cannot send, edit, or retry a
conversation message. Your choice does not remove your work from shared repositories or change other
members' choices. Research participation is a separate setting.

### Connect the accounts your workspace uses

A required link must match the Slack team or Outline instance configured for this workspace. A link
to a different team or instance does not satisfy it. Connecting an account identifies you on that
service; it does not enable AI.

**Continue** finishes setup once you have chosen an answer and connected the required accounts. Your
answer is saved when you select **Continue**, **Save AI choice**, or **Save and connect** for an account.
When required accounts are still open, **Save AI choice** saves your answer without finishing setup.
This includes **No AI**: you never need to connect an account to opt out. **Skip for now** leaves
without saving a new answer; an already saved answer stays in effect, and the page waits for you under
**Your AI choice** until a workspace owner changes the setup. On a return visit, **Save**
records a new answer whether or not the required accounts are connected, and **Back to workspace**
leaves without saving. If a required account is unavailable right
now, it does not hold you up; ask a workspace owner to repair it or remove the requirement. Optional
accounts never block completion. After finishing or skipping, you return to the workspace page you
originally opened, including its filters and selected section.

The sign-in notice and the research question are separate from workspace setup. Linking an account
or choosing an answer does not change research participation.

## Connected Accounts

Link the sign-in and project accounts you use. Linking requires a recent sign-in. A linked account
helps Hephaestus identify your work on that service. It does not grant a workspace membership or
allow AI processing. You cannot unlink your last sign-in account.

## Slack

For each connected Slack workspace, **Use my new channel messages** controls whether new messages
you write in monitored channels can be used as context. **Forget my mirrored messages** removes
the copies from Hephaestus in that workspace; it does not remove the messages from Slack.
Read [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#your-choices-and-right-to-object) before you use this control.

## Practice feedback

**Comments and Slack reminders** controls new feedback comments on work you author and reminders
about feedback prepared for a conversation with Heph. Turning it off does not stop reviews or
remove existing feedback. Your Practice profile remains available.
A Slack reminder is a direct message, at most once in 24 hours, when your workspace has connected
Slack and your Slack account is linked. The reminder does not contain the feedback text.

## Email notifications

Choose which optional emails you receive. Available choices depend on your role and the instance's
email setup. Survey invitations and research participation are separate choices.
Your contact address comes from a linked sign-in provider that supplied a verified email address;
you cannot type a different address here. See [Feedback and surveys](./product-feedback#email-invitations).

## Academic research participation

When the instance runs an academic study, this section lets you change your participation.
Choosing AI or linking an account does not change this answer. See
[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#signing-in-and-choosing-research-participation).

## Privacy

Choose whether to allow optional browser analytics and error monitoring. Essential sign-in storage
remains necessary. See [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy) for the data notice and retention policy.

## Active Sessions

This section lists your signed-in browsers and devices, including Chrome extension sessions.
Each row shows available sign-in, expiry, and network details. **This device** marks the current session.
Choose **Revoke** to sign another session out.

To sign out everywhere else, choose **Sign out N other sessions**, then confirm with
**Sign out others**. Your current session stays signed in. The other sessions must sign in again.
Use the account menu to sign out of the current session.

## Danger Zone

Under **Export my data**, select **Request export**. Wait until the export is ready, then download
it as a JSON file. If preparation fails, select **Retry export**. Keep the downloaded file private.
An export contains your personal data; it is not an instance backup.

**Delete account** signs you out on all devices and disables your account after confirmation.
Permanent deletion follows after a cooldown of about 48 hours. Enter the confirmation phrase shown
in the dialog; you cannot undo this request from here.
Do not use this action to leave one workspace or to sign out a device. Read
[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy) for erasure details and requests from people who have never signed in.

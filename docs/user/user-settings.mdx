1. Open **User settings** at the bottom of the sidebar.

These settings belong to your account.
Workspace administration is separate.

## Practice feedback

**Comments and Slack reminders** controls two things.
It controls new feedback comments on work you author.
It also controls reminders about feedback prepared for your conversations with Heph.

Turning it off does not stop reviews or remove existing feedback.
Your Practice profile stays available.
A Slack reminder is a direct message, sent at most once in 24 hours.
It needs a connected Slack workspace and a connected Slack account.
It contains no feedback text.

## Academic research participation

If your instance runs an academic study, this section lets you change your participation.
AI choices and connected accounts do not change this answer.
[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#signing-in-and-choosing-research-participation) explains the research decision.

## Your AI choice

On your first visit, a workspace can ask you to finish a setup, even though you are already a member.
The setup page has two parts: your AI choice and any Slack or Outline accounts the workspace requires.
Setup neither approves you nor creates your membership.

You answer the AI question once across all workspaces on this Hephaestus deployment.
Another workspace never asks it again.
Its setup page appears only if it requires an account you have not connected.
Your answer is a boundary, not a model selection.
Anything stricter also qualifies, and nothing switches you to a less strict answer.

The cards compare three answers on the same four rows:

|             | **In-house**                                               | **Cloud**                                                                                    | **No AI**                               |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **AI help** | Feedback and Heph                                          | Feedback and Heph                                                                            | No new feedback or Heph replies         |
| **Models**  | Only models your organization runs, usually smaller models | In-house models plus cloud models, usually the strongest available                           | None                                    |
| **Speed**   | Your organization’s capacity can make replies slower       | More capacity, usually faster                                                                | Not applicable                          |
| **Sent to** | Only systems your organization runs                        | Also a cloud provider your organization configured. Its agreement sets retention and access. | Nowhere. Sync and stored work continue. |

Each workspace sets up its own models within these answers.
If no model in the current workspace meets an answer, its card says **not set up here yet**.
Nothing runs for you for that purpose in that workspace.
Hephaestus never switches you to another answer.

You can wait for a workspace owner to add a model, or you can change your answer.

“Set up” describes configuration, not service health or a speed guarantee.
In-house models depend on your organization’s capacity and storage policies.
Cloud permission does not guarantee better models or faster replies.
A model mark names its maker, not its operator.
For example, a private endpoint does not automatically make an OpenAI model served by Azure in-house.

The Logos mark names the Logos service, not its operator.
An organization can run Logos itself or use a provider-operated deployment.

1. Ask a workspace owner who operates the deployment and what terms apply to your work.

In a workspace, each answer’s card shows the first model for a new request, its maker’s logo, and the service that receives requests.
If more models are ready, the card shows their count.
Below the cards, a list names each model for your selected answer with its declared data use.
The cards in User settings show no model or service.
Your answer applies to every workspace, and each workspace can use a different service.

On your first visit:

1. Choose an answer.
2. Connect any required accounts that are available.
3. Select **Save AI choice**.

No answer starts selected.
You can choose any answer, including one that no workspace has set up yet.
Added models, removed models, and new declarations never need another answer from you.
Your answer stays in force, and Hephaestus checks it on each request.

To change your answer at any time:

1. Open **Your AI choice** in a workspace sidebar or in **User settings**.
2. Choose an answer.
3. Select **Save AI choice**.

The workspace page also shows the accounts it asks you to connect and whether a model there meets your answer.
Existing feedback and earlier Heph conversations stay readable.
With **No AI**, no new practice reviews about you, Heph requests, or feedback delivery start.
Hephaestus cannot recall requests already sent.
You cannot send, edit, or retry a conversation message.

Your choice does not remove your work from shared repositories or change other members’ choices.
Research participation is separate.

### Connect the accounts your workspace uses

A required account must match the Slack team or Outline instance configured for this workspace.
An account on a different team or instance does not count.
Connecting an account identifies you on that service but does not turn on AI.

**Save AI choice** and **Save and connect** both save your answer.
On your first visit, **Save AI choice** also finishes setup once you have answered and connected every required account.
If required accounts are still unconnected, it saves your answer without finishing setup.
This holds for **No AI** too, because you never need to connect an account to opt out.

**Skip for now** leaves without saving a new answer.
An existing answer stays in effect.
The page stays under **Your AI choice** until a workspace owner changes the setup.
On return visits, **Save AI choice** records a new answer even if required accounts are unconnected.
**Back to workspace** leaves without saving.

An account that is unavailable right now does not block setup.
Optional accounts never block completion.
After you finish or skip, you return to the workspace page you came from, with its filters and selected section.

1. If a required account is unavailable, ask a workspace owner to repair it or remove the requirement.

The sign-in notice and the research question are separate from workspace setup.
Connected accounts and AI choices do not change research participation.

## Email notifications

The optional email choices you see depend on your role and on your instance’s email setup.
Survey invitations and research participation are separate choices.
Your contact address comes from a connected sign-in provider that supplied a verified email address.
You cannot enter a different address here.
[Feedback and surveys](./product-feedback#email-invitations) gives more information.

1. Choose which optional emails to receive.

## Connected accounts

Connecting an account needs a recent sign-in.
It helps Hephaestus identify your work on that service.
It grants neither workspace membership nor AI permission.
You cannot disconnect your last sign-in account.

1. Select **Connect** next to each sign-in and project account you use.

To stop using an account, select **Disconnect** next to it.

## Slack

For each connected Slack workspace, **Use your new channel messages** controls whether Hephaestus uses your new messages in monitored channels.
This context supports your Heph conversations.

**CAUTION:** **Stop using and delete** permanently deletes your collected channel-message data in that workspace.
To keep that collected data, do not confirm deletion.
It does not remove your messages from Slack.

1. Read [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#your-choices-and-right-to-object) before you use this control.
2. To stop collection, turn off **Use your new channel messages**.
3. In the confirmation dialog, select **Stop using and delete**.

This stops new collection and deletes the channel-message data already collected.

## Active sessions

This section lists the browsers and devices where you are signed in, including Chrome extension sessions.
Each row shows the available sign-in, expiry, and network details.
**This device** marks your current session.

1. To sign out another session, select **Sign out** on its row.

To sign out all other sessions:

1. Select **Sign out N other sessions**, where N is the number of other sessions.
2. In the dialog, select **Sign out other sessions**.

Your current session stays signed in.
Other sessions must sign in again.

1. To sign out the current session, use the account menu.

## Privacy

Essential sign-in storage stays on, because Hephaestus needs it.
[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy) gives the data notice and retention policy.

1. Select **Change cookie choices**.
2. Choose whether to allow optional error reports.

## Danger zone

To export your account data:

1. Under **Export your data**, select **Request export**.
2. If the export fails, select **Request export again**.
3. Wait until the export is ready.
4. Select **Download** to save the JSON file.
5. Keep the downloaded file private.

An export contains your personal data, not an instance backup.

**CAUTION:** **Delete account** disables your account and leads to permanent deletion after a cooldown of about 48 hours.
You cannot undo the request here.
Do not use it to leave one workspace or sign out a device.

1. Read [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy) for erasure details and for requests from people who have never signed in.
2. Select **Delete account**.
3. In the dialog, enter the confirmation phrase it shows.
4. Select **Delete account** again.

Confirming signs you out on all devices and disables your account.

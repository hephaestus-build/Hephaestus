1. Open **User settings** at the bottom of the sidebar.

These settings belong to your account.
Workspace administration is separate.

## Your AI choice

A workspace can require setup on your first visit, after you already have membership.
The setup page has two parts: your AI choice and any Slack or Outline accounts the workspace requires.
Setup neither approves nor creates membership.

You answer the AI question once across all workspaces on this Hephaestus deployment.
Another workspace never asks it again.
Its setup page appears only if it requires an account you have not connected.
Your answer is a boundary, not a model selection.
Anything stricter also qualifies, and nothing switches you to a less strict answer.

The cards compare three answers on the same four rows:

| | **In-house** | **Cloud** | **No AI** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AI help** | Feedback and Heph | Feedback and Heph | No new feedback or Heph replies |
| **Models** | Only models your organization runs, usually smaller models | In-house models plus cloud models, usually the strongest available | None |
| **Speed** | Your organization's capacity can make replies slower | More capacity, usually faster | Not applicable |
| **Sent to** | Only systems your organization runs | Also a cloud provider your organization configured. Its agreement sets retention and access. | Nowhere. Sync and stored work continue. |

Each workspace sets up its own models within these answers.
If no model meets an answer in the current workspace, its card says *not set up here yet*.
Nothing runs for you for that purpose in that workspace.
Hephaestus never switches you to another answer.

1. Wait for a workspace owner to add a model, or change your answer.

"Set up" describes configuration, not service health or a speed guarantee.
In-house models depend on your organization's capacity and storage policies.
Cloud permission does not guarantee better models or faster replies.
A model mark names its maker, not its operator.
For example, a private endpoint does not automatically make an OpenAI model served by Azure in-house.

The Logos mark names the Logos service, not its operator.
An organization can run Logos itself or use a provider-operated deployment.

1. Ask a workspace owner who operates the deployment and what terms apply to your work.

In a workspace, each answer's card shows the first model for a new request, its maker's logo, and the service that receives requests.
If more models are ready, the card shows their count.
Below the cards, a list names each model for your selected answer with its declared data use.
User settings cards show no model or service.
Your answer applies to each workspace, and each can use a different service.

On the first visit:

1. Choose an answer.
2. Connect any required accounts that are available.
3. Select **Continue**.

No answer starts selected.
On a return visit, the button reads **Save**.
You can choose any answer, including one no workspace has set up yet.
Model additions, removals, or new declarations never require another answer.
Your answer stays in force, and Hephaestus checks it on each request.

To change your answer at any time:

1. Open **Your AI choice** in a workspace sidebar or **User settings**.

The workspace page also shows its account links and whether a model there meets your answer.
Existing feedback and earlier Heph conversations stay readable.
With **No AI**, no new practice reviews about you, Heph requests, or feedback delivery start.
Hephaestus cannot recall requests already sent.
You cannot send, edit, or retry a conversation message.

Your choice does not remove work from shared repositories or change other members' choices.
Research participation is separate.

### Connect the accounts your workspace uses

A required link must match the Slack team or Outline instance configured for this workspace.
A link to a different team or instance does not qualify.
An account link identifies you on that service but does not enable AI.

**Continue** finishes setup after your AI answer and required account links are complete.
**Continue**, **Save AI choice**, and **Save and connect** for an account save your answer.
With required accounts still unconnected, **Save AI choice** saves your answer without completing setup.
This includes **No AI**: an account link is never required to opt out.

**Skip for now** leaves without saving a new answer.
An existing answer stays in effect.
The page stays under **Your AI choice** until a workspace owner changes the setup.
On return visits, **Save** records a new answer even with unconnected required accounts.
**Back to workspace** leaves without saving.

An account that is unavailable right now does not block setup.
Optional accounts never block completion.
After completion or a skip, you return to the original workspace page, with its filters and selected section.

1. If a required account is unavailable, ask a workspace owner to repair it or remove the requirement.

The sign-in notice and research question are separate from workspace setup.
Account links and AI choices do not change research participation.

## Connected Accounts

An account link requires a recent sign-in.
It helps Hephaestus identify your work on that service.
It grants neither workspace membership nor AI permission.
You cannot unlink your last sign-in account.

1. Link the sign-in and project accounts you use.

## Slack

For each connected Slack workspace, **Use my new channel messages** controls context from your new messages in monitored channels.
This context supports your Heph conversations.

**CAUTION:** **Turn off & delete** permanently deletes your collected channel-message data in that workspace.
To keep that collected data, do not confirm deletion.
It does not remove your messages from Slack.

1. Read [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#your-choices-and-right-to-object) before you use this control.
2. To stop collection, turn off **Use my new channel messages**.
3. In the confirmation dialog, select **Turn off & delete**.

This stops new collection and deletes already collected channel-message data.

## Practice feedback

**Comments and Slack reminders** controls new feedback comments on work you author and reminders about feedback prepared for Heph conversations.
Disabling it does not stop reviews or remove existing feedback.
Your Practice profile stays available.
A Slack reminder is a direct message, at most once in 24 hours.
It requires a connected Slack workspace and linked Slack account.
It contains no feedback text.

## Email notifications

Optional email choices depend on your role and the instance's email setup.
Survey invitations and research participation are separate choices.
Your contact address comes from a linked sign-in provider that supplied a verified email address.
You cannot enter a different address here.
[Feedback and surveys](./product-feedback#email-invitations) gives more information.

1. Choose which optional emails to receive.

## Academic research participation

If the instance runs an academic study, this section lets you change participation.
AI choices and account links do not change this answer.
[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy#signing-in-and-choosing-research-participation) explains the research decision.

## Privacy

Essential sign-in storage remains necessary.
[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy) gives the data notice and retention policy.

1. Choose whether to allow optional browser analytics and error monitoring.

## Active Sessions

This section lists signed-in browsers and devices, including Chrome extension sessions.
Each row shows available sign-in, expiry, and network details.
**This device** marks the current session.

1. To sign out another session, select **Revoke**.

To sign out all other sessions:

1. Select **Sign out N other sessions**.
2. Confirm with **Sign out others**.

Your current session stays signed in.
Other sessions must sign in again.

1. To sign out the current session, use the account menu.

## Danger Zone

To export account data:

1. Under **Export my data**, select **Request export**.
2. If preparation fails, select **Retry export**.
3. Wait until the export is ready.
4. Download the JSON file.
5. Keep the downloaded file private.

An export contains personal data, not an instance backup.

**CAUTION:** **Delete account** disables your account and leads to permanent deletion after a cooldown of about 48 hours.
You cannot undo the request here.
Do not use it to leave one workspace or sign out a device.

1. Read [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy) for erasure details and requests from people who have never signed in.
2. Select **Delete**.
3. Enter the confirmation phrase shown in the dialog.
4. Select **Delete account**.

Confirmation signs you out on all devices and disables your account.

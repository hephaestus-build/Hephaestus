Practices across the workspace shows where the developers in your workspace stand in each practice group.
It never names a developer: other people appear only as counts.
Each standing comes from AI review of the work and can be wrong.
Your own standings and next steps are on your [Practice profile](./practice-profile.mdx).

The page appears only in a workspace that reviews practices.
Its explanations are behind the info buttons (i).

## Your figures

Four tiles set your own figure beside the typical range of the workspace.
The typical range is the middle half of developers, from the 25th to the 75th percentile.
A blue marker shows your value.

- **Pieces of work reviewed**, **Practices going well**, and **Practices needing attention** count within the time range that you select.
  Select **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, or **All time**.
- **Open feedback** counts the feedback that is open, whatever the time range.

The time-range tiles count the developers who have a standing in that range.

## The bars

**All practice groups** has one bar for each practice group.
Each bar counts the developers with a standing, by the standing that their Practice profile shows.
Each developer counts once, however much of their work was reviewed.
The bars show the current standings, so the time range does not change them.

The **You** bracket spans the part of the bar that you are in, when the bar counts you.
It marks the part, not a point within it.

The colors of the group names only tell the groups apart. They are not a rating.

A practice that a person checks by hand, or that nobody reviews, has no standing and is not on the page.
Turning feedback off for a practice does not remove its standing.

If some standings include assessments from earlier reviews, the page says so.
If your standing includes them, your row reads **Based on earlier reviews, the criteria might have changed**.

## Small counts

The page shows every count, however small, and everyone sees the same counts.
A small count can tell other members where you stand:

- A small part of a bar can identify one or two developers to a member who knows them.
- A typical range of one or two developers can show their values.
- A member who compares the page before and after your next review can see the counts change.

The page does not count hidden members.
A workspace admin can hide you under **Members**.

## Open the page

1. In the sidebar, under **Workspace**, select **Practices across the workspace**.
2. Optional: Select a time range for the tiles.

## Open a group

1. Select **Open group**.
   A panel shows the bar of the group and a bar for each of its practices.
2. To see your own standing in the group, select **See your own standing** at the foot of the panel.
   To go to one practice in your profile, select **Open in your Practice profile** in its row.

At the end of the page, **See your own standing** opens **All practice groups** in your Practice profile.

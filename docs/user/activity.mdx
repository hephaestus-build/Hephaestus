Activity shows the work you and your workspace already do on GitHub or GitLab: what is waiting on you,
what you have open, and what happened lately. It counts and lists that work; it never scores the work
or ranks anyone.

There are two pages, both in the sidebar under **Dashboards**:

- **Activity** is your own page. It is also the page a workspace opens on.
- **Workspace activity** shows everyone in the workspace, or one team.

Every workspace has both pages; there is nothing to turn on. They show the work in the workspace's
repositories. Everyone with a role in the workspace sees it, regardless of what each of them can see
on GitHub or GitLab. On GitLab, pages say *merge request* where this guide says *pull request*.

## Your Activity

- **Waiting on you** lists open pull requests that ask for your review.
- **Your open pull requests** lists the pull requests you opened that are still open, drafts
  included. Each one shows whether it is a **Draft**, whether a reviewer asked for **Changes
  requested**, whether it is **Approved**, and whether **Checks failing** on its latest commit.
  Requesting changes on a merge request is a GitLab Premium feature; without it, GitLab work never
  shows **Changes requested** and no change requests are counted.
- **Assigned to you** lists open issues assigned to you.
- **Summary** counts what you did in a time range: the last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or 12 months.
- **Recent activity** lists what you did in that range, newest first and grouped by day.

When your workspace reviews practices, a link at the end of the page opens your
[Practice profile](./practice-profile).

Every item opens the work on GitHub or GitLab, where you act on it. Hephaestus does not change
anything there.

### The summary

The summary has one row for each kind of work:

| Row | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Pull requests** | Pull requests you opened, and those of yours that were merged or closed without merging |
| **Reviews** | Your reviews of other people's pull requests: approvals, change requests and reviews with comments only |
| **Issues** | Issues you opened, and those of yours that were closed |
| **Comments** | Your comments in the conversation of a pull request or issue, and your comments on code |

Open a row to see the activity behind its count; the count and the list follow the same rules.

A few rules decide who a count belongs to:

- **A merge counts for the pull request's author**, whoever pressed the button. The same goes for a
  pull request closed without merging. When the author is unknown, it counts for nobody.
- **A closed issue counts for the issue's author**, whoever closed it.
- **A review of your own pull request is not counted.** A review is something you do for someone
  else's work. Your comments on your own pull request do count as comments.
- **Bots are not counted.**

A count says how much of something happened, not how good the work was. Planning, pairing and
helping in a chat do not show up here.

## Workspace activity

Workspace activity shows the same kinds of counts for everyone. Use **Team** to narrow it to one
team; **Everyone** shows the whole workspace. A team includes its sub-teams, and its activity is
counted in the repositories that team's settings include. Where a team filters reviews by label,
only reviews of pull requests with one of those labels count for it. Workspace totals count only
current members.

- **Summary** counts the workspace's or the team's activity in the time range, and says how many
  members were active.
- **Members** lists the members by name. Open a member to see what is waiting on their review, what
  they have open, their summary and their recent activity, with a link to their GitHub or GitLab
  profile. With a team selected, a member's summary and activity are that team's, so they match
  the member's row.
- **Recent activity** lists what happened, with who did it.

A workspace admin can hide a member or a team from workspace activity. A hidden member is left out
of every count and list here. A hidden team is not offered in **Team** and does not count toward the
team above it. Hiding someone does not change their own Activity page.

# Product feedback and surveys

Product feedback helps your instance's administrators improve Hephaestus. It is separate from
**practice feedback**, which Hephaestus gives you about your work.

## Send product feedback

Open **Product feedback** in the header and choose **Send feedback** or **Report a bug**. Describe what
worked, what went wrong, or what would help. For a bug, include what you were trying to do, what
happened, and what you expected.

**Include page and browser details** attaches the page path and the browser identification shown in
the dialog, so a bug can be reproduced. It starts ticked for a bug report and unticked for
feedback; you can change it either way. Query parameters, page contents and logs are never attached.

A successful send clears the draft and shows an acknowledgement. A failed send keeps the draft so
you can try again. Wait a minute between feedback submissions.

## Answer a survey

Open surveys are listed under **Product feedback** in the header, with how many questions they have
and roughly how long they take. The count on the button tells you something is waiting. Each new
survey is announced once with a short notice that never opens the survey by itself.

The survey asks one question at a time and shows where you are. **Next** needs an answer; an
optional question also offers **Skip**, which on the last question is **Skip and send**.
**Previous** goes back. Some choice questions take another answer you type in — instead of an
option for single choice, in addition to them for multiple choice. Number or letter keys pick a
choice where the survey shows them, Enter continues, and in a free-text answer Enter starts a new
line while Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac) continues. Closing the dialog keeps your draft and the
question you were on. Refreshing, leaving the workspace, or signing out clears it.

**Decline survey** removes the invitation for your account across devices. Use **Undo** in the
acknowledgement if you declined by mistake. Closing the dialog is not a decline. A survey cannot be
answered or restored once it is paused, ended, or deleted.

## Privacy and help

Feedback and survey responses are linked to your account and visible to this instance's
administrators. They are not anonymous and are not sent to the Hephaestus project. Product
surveys are not research surveys, whatever you chose about research participation. Do not include
secrets or personal data about others.

Contact your instance administrator to object to processing or request removal of a submission.
See [Privacy](privacy.mdx) for your instance's privacy information. Use your established support or
security-reporting channel for urgent or confidential reports.

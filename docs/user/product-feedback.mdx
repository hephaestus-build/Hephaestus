# Product feedback and surveys

Product feedback helps your instance's administrators improve Hephaestus. It is separate from
**practice feedback**, which Hephaestus gives you about your work.

## Send product feedback

Use **Send product feedback** in the header. Choose **Feedback** or **Bug report**, then describe
what worked, what went wrong, or what would help. For a bug, include what you were trying to do,
what happened, and what you expected.

To attach the current page path, review the displayed path and select **Include current page path**.
This is off by default; query parameters, page contents and logs are not attached.

A successful send clears the draft and shows an acknowledgement. A failed send keeps the draft so
you can try again. Wait a minute between feedback submissions.

## Answer a survey

**Surveys** in the header lists invitations for your workspace, including surveys for all workspaces.
Choose **Take survey** when you are ready; invitations do not open automatically.

Required questions are marked. You can skip optional questions or clear an optional choice.
Closing the dialog or returning to the list keeps your draft. Refreshing, leaving the workspace,
or signing out clears it.

**Decline this survey** removes the invitation for your account across devices. Use **Undo** in the
acknowledgement if you declined by mistake. Closing the dialog is not a decline. A survey cannot be answered
or restored while paused or expired.

## Privacy and help

Feedback and survey responses are linked to your account and visible to this instance's
administrators. They are not anonymous or automatically sent to the Hephaestus project. Product
surveys are not research surveys. Do not include secrets or sensitive personal data.

Contact your instance administrator to object to processing or request removal of a submission.
See [Privacy](privacy.mdx) for your instance's privacy information. Use your established support or
security-reporting channel for urgent or confidential reports.

# Feedback and surveys

The **Feedback** button in the header is the one place to talk to the Hephaestus team. It is
separate from **practice feedback**, which Hephaestus gives you about your work.

## Share an idea, report a bug, send feedback

Choose **Share an idea**, **Report a bug** or **Send feedback**. The dialog asks for the one thing
that kind needs: what you would change, what happened, or what works and what gets in your way. You
can switch kind inside the dialog without losing what you typed.

What you send goes straight to the Hephaestus team, with your name so they can follow up. If you
would rather discuss something in the open, **Open an issue on GitHub** in the same menu takes you
to the public issue tracker.

**Attach the page and browser you're on** adds the page path and the browser identification shown
in the dialog, so a bug can be reproduced. It starts ticked for a bug report and unticked otherwise.
Query parameters, page contents and logs are never attached.

A successful send clears the draft and thanks you. A failed send keeps the draft and says why.
Closing the dialog keeps the draft until you reload, switch workspace or sign out. Wait a minute
between sends.

## Answer a survey

Surveys wait behind the same button, each with how many questions it has and roughly how long it
takes; a count on the button says something is waiting. A new survey is announced once with a short
notice that never opens the survey by itself.

Before the first question, the survey says who reads the answers. A **product** survey is read by
the Hephaestus team and is not research. A survey marked **Research** is part of the study run by
the organisation named when you were asked about research participation, is offered only while you
take part in it, and its answers are that study's data, kept apart from product feedback. You can leave the study at any time in **User settings**; answers you already sent
stay with the study.

The survey asks one question at a time. **Next** needs an answer; a question marked *(optional)*
also offers **Skip**, which on the last question is **Skip and send**. **Previous** goes back. Some
choice questions take **Something else** you type in — instead of an option for single choice, in
addition to them for multiple choice. Number or letter keys pick a choice where the survey shows
them, Enter continues, and in a free-text answer Enter starts a new line while Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter
on a Mac) continues. Closing the dialog keeps your draft and the question you were on; reloading,
leaving the workspace or signing out clears it.

**Decline survey** removes the invitation for your account across devices. Use **Undo** in the
notice if you declined by mistake. Closing the dialog is not a decline. A survey cannot be answered
or restored once it is paused, ended or deleted.

## Privacy and help

Feedback and survey answers are linked to your account. They are not anonymous. Do not include
secrets or personal data about others.

Use the privacy contact in **Privacy** to object to processing or request removal of a submission.
See [Privacy](privacy.mdx) for your instance's privacy information. Use your established support or
security-reporting channel for urgent or confidential reports.

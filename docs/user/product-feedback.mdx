Use **Product feedback** in the header to report a bug or suggest a change.
Submissions reach your instance admins.
This is separate from **practice feedback**, which Hephaestus gives you about your work.

## Share an idea, report a bug, send feedback

1. Select **Product feedback** in the header.
2. Select **Share an idea**, **Report a bug**, or **Send feedback**.
3. Enter the details the dialog asks for.
4. Choose whether to include your page and browser with **Attach the page and browser you’re on**.
5. Select **Send**.

Wait one minute before you send another submission.
For a bug, describe the action, what happened, and what you expected.
For example: “I selected Copy as Markdown on Activity, but the clipboard was empty.”

You can change the kind of submission in the dialog without losing your text.
Your submission goes directly to instance admins, with your name and contact address so they can follow up.
To discuss in public instead, select **Open an issue on GitHub** in the same menu.
It opens the public issue tracker.

**Attach the page and browser you’re on** adds the page and the browser identification that the dialog shows.
The page is the screen’s route, such as `/w/$workspaceSlug/activity`, not its address.
So it does not include your workspace’s name or the IDs in the address.
This helps reproduce a bug.
It starts selected for bug reports and cleared otherwise.
It never attaches query parameters, page contents, or logs.

If the submission goes through, the dialog clears your draft and says thanks.
If it does not, the dialog keeps your draft and explains why.
If you close the dialog, the draft stays until you reload, switch workspace, or sign out.

## Who can read your submission

Your product feedback links to your account. It is not anonymous.
Instance admins can read it in their private inbox.
Submissions stay on your instance and have no automatic expiry by age.
Account erasure and removal of the workspace you sent from remove the corresponding submissions.
See [Privacy](./privacy) for your rights and your instance’s privacy contact.

Heph is the conversational AI mentor in Hephaestus. It can use your recent work and the practice feedback you received as context, so you do not need to recap everything before asking a question.

Heph is one part of the Hephaestus platform. Automated practice feedback on pull requests, merge requests, and issues is described separately in [Practice feedback](./ai-code-review).

## Who can use Heph

Heph is available to every member of a workspace where a workspace admin has turned on **Chat with
Heph** under **Administration → Settings**. You don't need to request access or sign in again once
it is on. In each workspace:

- **Heph is not in the navigation, or a link to it says Heph is off in this workspace:** ask a
  workspace admin to turn it on.
- **Heph says it is off for you:** you chose **No AI**. Change **Your AI choice** to use Heph again.
- **Heph asks you to choose which AI may handle your work:** this workspace needs your AI choice
  first.
- **Heph says it isn't set up for your AI choice yet:** no Heph model in this workspace is within your
  choice. Ask a workspace owner to add one, or change your choice.

Slack direct messages follow the same rules. When Heph is off in the workspace, it does not reply.
In the other three cases it replies with the same reason and starts no conversation. See
[Your AI choice](./workspace#your-ai-choice).

## Start a chat

1. Open **Heph**.
2. Enter a question or describe what you want to reflect on.
3. Continue the conversation, or select **New chat** when you want a separate thread.

Previous chats appear in the Heph sidebar. Select a thread to continue it.

## What Heph can use as context

Depending on your workspace configuration, Heph can use:

- the pull requests, merge requests, and issues you recently authored, plus how much you have reviewed
- for your open pull requests and merge requests, as Hephaestus last recorded them: whether GitHub or GitLab
  reported them as mergeable, their latest pipeline or check result, each reviewer's latest review, and the general
  comments and inline review threads on them. Neither an approval nor a resolved thread shows that a condition
  written in a comment or thread was met. This record can lag GitHub or GitLab, so Heph says what it could not
  confirm and asks you to check there before you merge
- your most recent practice feedback: what Hephaestus recorded as delivered to you, whether feedback prepared for
  your practice page has been recorded as opened, and whether Hephaestus recorded that delivering a piece of feedback
  failed. This record shows Heph text only for feedback recorded as delivered on your work or your practice page,
  never for conversations, and not your full feedback history; a private coaching brief prepared for Heph reaches it
  separately, as described below. A practice that keeps going well shows as going well on your practice page without
  producing private feedback there, so Heph does not treat the absence of feedback as a failed delivery
- the engineering practices configured for your workspace
- messages from Slack channels that the workspace monitors and you have allowed Hephaestus to use
- selected team documents from a connected Outline workspace

Heph uses this context to answer your question. It does not have an unrestricted checkout of every connected repository, and it may ask for a specific snippet when the available context is not enough.

Heph does not use work that was deleted on GitHub or GitLab. Your leaderboard score, profile counts, and the practice feedback you already received are unaffected: they record what happened at the time.

A practice review can also prepare a private coaching brief for Heph. The brief contains a situation,
the capability to explore, an evidence summary, and something that can be observed in the conversation.
It is not a queued reply and does not start a chat or send a notification. When you next talk to Heph,
the mentor receives the brief, the authorized observation evidence behind it, and the live conversation;
it decides whether to ask, explain, or leave the point alone.

## Questions to try

- "What feedback did I get on my latest pull request?"
- "I disagree with this comment. Can we work through it?"
- "Which recent change would be useful to reflect on?"
- "What could I try differently on my next review?"
- "Summarise what we decided in this conversation."

You do not need to use a template or follow a fixed session. Ask one concrete question and take the conversation where it is useful.

## Check the answer

Heph can misunderstand your work or repeat a mistaken practice observation. Treat its answers as suggestions, not decisions.

If an answer does not match the work:

1. say what looks wrong
2. point Heph to the relevant comment, line, or explanation
3. ask it to reconsider using that evidence

The developer and human reviewers remain responsible for decisions about the work.

## Use Heph in Slack

When a workspace admin connects Slack, you can message Heph in a direct message. Slack direct messages and web-app chats have separate conversation histories.

Workspace admins can separately enable monitored channels as context. Monitoring starts only after Hephaestus posts a visible announcement, and individual privacy controls apply. See [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy.mdx#your-choices-and-right-to-object) for the Slack privacy controls and what they do not erase.

**Learn from the work you already do.** Hephaestus is an open-source AI mentor for software teams.
It reads your issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions against the practices that matter to your project.
It explains what went well, what could be better, and how to improve.
You can ask why, disagree, or decide on the next step together.

This guide is for developers who use Hephaestus and workspace admins who set it up.
The **Admin Guide** covers deployment.
The **Contributor Guide** covers development.

## What Hephaestus offers

### Practice feedback

- AI reads pull requests, merge requests, issues, and their conversations against the practices configured for your workspace.
- Each piece of feedback names its practice and points to evidence in your work.
- Feedback explains what went well, what could be better, and how to improve.
- Feedback on the work waits for a workspace admin's approval by default.
- Feedback reaches you on the work, on your practice pages, or in your next conversation with Hephaestus.

### Talking it through

- In chat, Hephaestus uses the name **Heph**.
- Heph uses recent project activity, your feedback, team conversations, and documentation as context.
- Heph is available in the web app and, when connected, in a Slack direct message.

### Practice profile

Your [Practice profile](./practice-profile) is the workspace home when it reviews practices.
It shows how your work stands, the evidence behind it, and your private feedback.
A workspace that does not review practices opens on Activity.

### Activity

- Activity shows work that needs your review, your open work, and your recent work.
- Workspace activity shows work for the workspace or one team, with each member listed by name.
- Activity counts and lists work, with links to GitHub or GitLab. See [Activity](./activity).

### Project context

Connected GitHub, GitLab, Slack, and Outline integrations supply project activity, team conversations, and documentation.
This context can help Hephaestus understand the work.

### Workspace management

Workspace admins can:

- Connect GitHub or GitLab repositories and keep each team's data separate.
- Adopt practices from the instance catalog and rewrite them for the project.
- Choose the AI model and set a monthly spending cap.
- Manage members, teams, and integrations.

## Your data and privacy

[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy.mdx) explains what connected services share and how AI uses it.
It also explains how long copies stay and how to request access, correction, or erasure.
This includes people who have never signed in.

## Terms used in this guide

- **Engineering practice:** an observable way of working. Examples include changes that are easy to review and explanations of their purpose.
- **Practice feedback:** feedback about engineering practices, with evidence from software project work.
- **Heph:** the name for Hephaestus in chat, in the web app or Slack.
- **Workspace:** the repositories, members, teams, practice catalog, and settings for one group.
- **Workspace admin:** a member who configures a workspace. An **instance admin** operates the whole deployment.

## Quick start

1. Sign in with a provider that your deployment offers.
2. Choose a workspace you belong to.
3. On your first visit, if asked, choose **In-house**, **Cloud**, or **No AI**.
4. Connect any required accounts.
5. Open your **Practice profile**, or **Activity** if this workspace does not review practices.
6. Work as usual in a connected GitHub or GitLab repository.
7. If practice reviews are on, open feedback from a contribution.
8. Ask a follow-up question about feedback you receive.

You can change your AI answer in **User settings**.
[Getting started](./getting-started) gives a tour of the current navigation.

Hephaestus helps you improve engineering practices through feedback on your existing project work.
You work in GitHub or GitLab as usual.
Hephaestus reviews eligible work against your workspace practices and can give feedback with evidence.
You can discuss that feedback with **Heph**, the conversational assistant.

Start with [Getting started](./getting-started) to sign in and open your workspace.
If you already use Hephaestus, choose a task below.
The **Admin Guide** covers instance operation and workspace setup.
The **Contributor Guide** covers development.

## What Hephaestus offers

### Practice feedback

Read feedback about a pull request, merge request, or issue.
Each piece names its practice and points to evidence in your work.
Feedback on the work waits for a workspace admin's approval by default.
Other feedback appears on your practice pages or in a conversation with Heph.
[Practice feedback](./ai-code-review) explains how to read it, respond, and ask for another review.

### Talking it through

Ask Heph about feedback, a review request, or a next step for your work.
It can use permitted workspace context, so you do not need to repeat every detail.
[Chat with Heph](./ai-mentor) explains how to start in the web app or Slack.

### Practice profile

Your [Practice profile](./practice-profile) is the workspace home when it reviews practices.
It shows how your work stands, the evidence behind it, and your private feedback.
A workspace that does not review practices opens on Activity.

### Activity

Find pull requests that need your review, check your assigned issues, or copy recent work for a standup.
Workspace activity shows work for the workspace or one team, with members listed by name.
[Activity](./activity) explains both pages.
They count work without ranking people.

### Project context

Connected GitHub, GitLab, Slack, and Outline integrations supply project activity, team conversations, and documentation.
This context can help Hephaestus understand the work.

### Workspace management

Workspace admins can:

- Connect GitHub or GitLab repositories and keep each team's data separate.
- Adopt practices from the instance catalog and rewrite them for the project.
- Choose the AI model and set a monthly spending cap.
- Manage members, teams, and integrations.

## Your data and privacy

[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy.mdx) explains what connected services share and how AI uses it.
It also explains how long copies stay and how to request access, correction, or erasure.
This includes people who have never signed in.

## Terms used in this guide

- **Engineering practice:** an observable way of working. Examples include changes that are easy to review and explanations of their purpose.
- **Practice feedback:** feedback about engineering practices, with evidence from software project work.
- **Heph:** the name for Hephaestus in chat, in the web app or Slack.
- **Workspace:** the repositories, members, teams, practice catalog, and settings for one group.
- **Workspace admin:** a member who configures a workspace. An **instance admin** operates the whole deployment.

## Quick start

1. Follow [Getting started](./getting-started) to sign in and complete any required setup.
2. Open **Practice profile** to read feedback, or **Activity** to find work that needs you.
3. For a question about your work or feedback, open **Heph**.

Your workspace determines which AI features are set up.
You can change your AI choice in **User settings**.

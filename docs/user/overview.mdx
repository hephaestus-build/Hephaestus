Hephaestus helps software teams put good engineering practices into their everyday work.
It reviews pull requests, issues, code reviews and team discussions as the work happens.
Each person gets feedback that cites their work and names a next step.
Feedback appears on the work while it is still open, in a weekly digest, and on each person’s Practice profile.
The profile shows how each practice develops over time.
Work drafted with a coding agent gets the same feedback.

The feedback helps you practice.
It is not a grade, an appraisal, or an input to allocation or pay.
Unless a workspace owner turns on per-person results, workspace admins see practice results with no names and no filter by person.
Each result still shows the work it is about.
[Privacy](./privacy#who-can-see-what) explains who sees what.

You work in GitHub or GitLab as usual.
You can ask Hephaestus about your feedback and recent work.

## Start here

[Getting started](./getting-started) takes you from sign-in to your first feedback.
It also sets your AI choice, which decides which AI features work for you.
You can change your AI choice later in **User settings**.

## Practice feedback

Read feedback about a pull request, merge request, or issue.
Each piece names its practice and points to evidence in your work.
Hephaestus records observations before it prepares feedback.
Workspace admins can approve feedback before it appears on the work.
[Practice feedback](./ai-code-review) explains how to read it. [Practice profile](./practice-profile#respond-to-feedback) explains how to respond and ask for another review.

## Practice profile

Your [Practice profile](./practice-profile) is the home page of a workspace that reviews practices.
It shows how your work stands, the evidence behind it, and your private feedback.
A workspace that does not review practices opens on Activity.

## Ask Hephaestus

Ask Hephaestus about feedback, a review request, or a next step for your work.
You can ask why, ask for an example, or disagree.
Hephaestus can use the workspace context it’s allowed to see, so you do not need to repeat every detail.
[Ask Hephaestus](./ask-hephaestus) explains how to start in the web app or Slack.

## Activity

Find pull requests that need your review, check your assigned issues, or copy recent work for a standup.
Workspace activity lists people by name. You can see who contributed to the workspace or to one team.
[Activity](./activity) explains both pages.

## Chrome extension

Use the optional [Chrome extension](./browser-extension) to see practice reviews on GitHub or GitLab work pages.

## Workspaces and connected services

A workspace holds one team’s repositories, members, practices, integrations, and settings.
Your connected GitHub, GitLab, Slack, and Outline integrations supply project activity, team conversations, and documentation.
Hephaestus uses Slack channel messages only when you allow it.
This context can help Hephaestus understand the work.

Workspace admins can:

- Connect GitHub or GitLab repositories and keep each team’s data separate.
- Adopt practices from the instance catalog and customize them for the project.
- Choose the AI models, and set a monthly cap on the workspace’s own AI provider.
- Manage members, teams, and integrations.

[Set up your workspace](./workspace) explains these tasks.

## Terms used in this guide

- **Practice:** a way of working that a review can observe. Examples are changes that are easy to review and explanations of their purpose.
- **Practice feedback:** feedback on how you apply engineering practices, with evidence from your project work.
- **Observation:** the record that one review makes about one practice in one piece of your work.
- **Practice group:** a named set of related practices.
- **Standing:** **Going well**, **Mixed feedback**, or **Needs attention**, read from your newest reviewed work on a practice.
- **Ask Hephaestus:** the area where you talk about your work in the web app.
- **Workspace:** the repositories, members, teams, workspace practices, and settings for one group.
- **Workspace admin:** a member who configures a workspace. An **instance admin** operates the whole deployment.

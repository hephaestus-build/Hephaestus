**Learn from the work you already do.** Hephaestus is an open-source AI mentor for software teams.
It checks your issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions against the practices that matter to your project.
It tells you what went well, what could be better, and how to improve.
You can ask why, disagree, or decide on the next step together.

This guide is for developers who use Hephaestus and for workspace admins who set it up.
The **Admin Guide** covers deployment.
The **Contributor Guide** covers development.

## What Hephaestus offers

### Practice feedback

- Hephaestus checks your pull requests, merge requests, issues, and their conversations against your workspace’s practices.
- Each piece of feedback names its practice and points to evidence in your work.
- Feedback explains what went well, what could be better, and how to improve.
- By default, feedback on the work waits for a workspace admin’s approval.
- Feedback reaches you on the work, on your practice pages, or in your next conversation with Heph.

### Talking it through

- **Heph** is the assistant you talk to in chat.
- Heph uses recent project activity, your feedback, team conversations, and documentation as context.
- You can talk to Heph in the web app and, once you connect Slack, in a Slack direct message.

### Practice profile

Your [Practice profile](./practice-profile) is the home page of a workspace that reviews practices.
It shows how your work stands, the evidence behind it, and your private feedback.
A workspace that does not review practices opens on Activity.

### Activity

- Activity shows the work that needs you, your open work, and your recent work.
- Workspace activity shows the same for the workspace or one team, with each member listed by name.
- Activity counts and lists work, with links to GitHub or GitLab. See [Activity](./activity).

### Project context

Your connected GitHub, GitLab, Slack, and Outline integrations supply project activity, team conversations, and documentation.
This context helps Hephaestus understand your work.

### Workspace management

Workspace admins can:

- Connect GitHub or GitLab repositories and keep each team’s data separate.
- Adopt practices from the instance catalog and customize them for the project.
- Choose the AI model and set a monthly spending cap.
- Manage members, teams, and integrations.

## Your data and privacy

[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy.mdx) explains what connected services share and how AI uses it.
It also explains how long copies stay and how to request access, correction, or erasure.
This applies to people who have never signed in too.

## Terms used in this guide

- **Practice:** a way of working that a review can observe. Examples are changes that are easy to review and explanations of their purpose.
- **Practice feedback:** guidance about your practices, with evidence from your project work.
- **Heph:** the assistant you talk to in the web app or in Slack.
- **Workspace:** the repositories, members, teams, practice catalog, and settings for one group.
- **Workspace admin:** a member who sets up a workspace. An **instance admin** runs the whole deployment.

## Quick start

1. Sign in with a provider that your deployment offers.
2. Select a workspace you belong to.
3. If asked on your first visit, choose **In-house**, **Cloud**, or **No AI**.
4. Connect any accounts the workspace requires.
5. Open your **Practice profile**, or **Activity** if the workspace does not review practices.
6. Work as usual in a connected GitHub or GitLab repository.
7. If practice reviews are on, open the feedback on one of your contributions.
8. Ask Heph a follow-up question about that feedback.

You can change your AI choice in **User settings**.
[Getting started](./getting-started) gives a tour of the navigation.

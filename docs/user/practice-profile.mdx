Your Practice profile shows where your recent work stands on the practices your workspace cares about,
what held and what changed since the review before the latest one, and the feedback written from it.
Other developers cannot see it; [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy.mdx#ai-processing-and-visibility)
lists who else can. It rests on practice feedback about your own pull requests, merge requests,
issues, conversations and documents, never on comparisons with other developers.

## Opening it

Sign in and select a workspace: a workspace that reviews practices opens on your Practice profile. It
is also the first entry in the sidebar under Dashboards. A workspace that does not review practices
has no Practice profile and opens on [Activity](./activity.mdx) instead.

## Reading the top of the page

The header says what Hephaestus reviews and how a piece of feedback resolves. The chip beside the
title reads **Latest review** with the date of the latest review of your work and the work it
reviewed, or says that it stopped before it finished. When that review is still running, the chip reads
**Review running** and names the work instead. Either way, it opens
[Reviews of your work](#reviews-of-your-work). A review counts here only once it records something
about your work, so one that has not yet does not show on the chip or in the list. Before your first
review, it reads **No review yet**.
A ring counts your practices by standing — going well, mixed feedback, needs attention, nothing to
report yet, or not observed yet — and **See all practice groups** opens the table of every practice
group.

Under the header, Heph sums up what happened since the review before the latest one:

- **What is holding up well** — the practices you kept showing, each with how many pieces of work it
  held across, and any feedback your work resolved.
- **What needs your attention** — at most two practices you can do something about today: feedback
  your work had started to answer and a newer review raised again, and feedback that is new. Each
  row says what happened and on which piece of work, and **Read the feedback** takes you straight to
  that card below.
- **What changed** — a practice or group that moved to another standing, one seen for the first
  time, a trend that turned. The paragraph names the most important moves and counts the rest;
  **Show the changes** unfolds them.
- The work the latest review looked at, listed by kind.

The summary describes recent evidence, not your ability. There are no scores, no percentages and no
ranking.

## Reviews of your work

The chip beside the title opens the reviews that recorded something about your work, newest first and
grouped by day. A review appears here once it records something about your work, so one that is still
running may not be listed yet. The newest review is tagged **Latest**, and one somebody asked for is
tagged **Requested**. Each row names the work, linked to the original, and leads with the practices
the review found to improve: the first two by name, then how many more. Where it found none, the row
says *Nothing to improve*, or that the review stopped early or is still running. Under that, it counts
practices, each once: those that held, those that did not apply to the work and those it left
undecided. It then says how many practices the review reached, which also counts the practices where
it recorded nothing about you. Where the review left feedback for you on GitLab, **Read the feedback
on the merge request** opens the comment itself; elsewhere the row says how many pieces of feedback
reached you.

**Show** narrows the list to one kind of work, and **Timeframe** to the last 7, 30 or 90 days or
**All time**, where it starts. While either narrows the list, no row is tagged **Latest**, since the
newest review it keeps need not be your latest. **View earlier reviews** at the end of the list loads
the reviews before them. Once the whole list is loaded and nothing narrows it, a later review of the
same work says which one it is, such as *2nd review*. Where you may ask for another review of the
work — as its author or assignee, or as a workspace admin — **Review this now** sits beside the row.

**Open review** opens one review: the work, linked to the original, the review's date, how many of
its practices it reached, and **Review this now** where you may ask. Two tabs follow.
**Every practice** lists the practices behind this review: for each, what it saw in your work, one
line per observation, or why it stayed quiet; while the review is still running, practices appear
as it reaches them. **Group** narrows it to one practice group,
**Reviews when** to the practices that review on one moment, such as new commits pushed, and
**Practice** to the names you type. **What we noticed** lists in order everything recorded about the
work: what happened, how it was noticed, and whether a review started.

## Your feedback

Each card is one pattern that recurred across your work: what was seen, on which pieces of work and
when, and one next step to try. The tabs above the cards split them, newest first: **Newest** shows
the two newest open cards, **Open** every open card, **Resolved and closed** every card that resolved
or closed, and **All** every card, withdrawn ones included.

A card resolves in one of two ways:

- **By your work.** Once three pieces of your work in a row come back clean on the practice, the card
  reads *Resolved by the work* and names the pieces of work that did it. A piece of work that shows the
  problem again starts the count over; the line under the next step keeps the count.
- **By you.** Under **Your response**, choose **Addressed** or **Not applicable** on the card; it then
  reads *Marked as addressed* or *Marked as not applicable* with the day you did. Press it again to
  take it back, and the card opens again.

If your workspace changes a practice after a card about it was written, the card closes without
resolving: it reads *Closed*, gives the day and the reason, and stops counting clean work. A closed
card stays on the page for 30 days after it closed. A newer card about the same practice replaces the
open one, so you never have two open cards about one practice.

A workspace admin can withdraw a card whose words were wrong. If you had already seen it, a recent
withdrawal shows under **All** as a short notice that it was withdrawn, without what it said; one you had not
seen yet simply never appears. A withdrawn card takes no new answer. It resolved nothing and says nothing about how
your work stands, and what the reviews observed is unchanged. What it said is left out of the feedback given
to later reviews and Heph. If the admin restores it, it comes back as it was.

Rate a card **Helpful** or **Not helpful**. Helpful opens an optional note; Not helpful asks for a reason
and a sentence on what was off, so the feedback can improve. Choosing **Not accurate** as the reason
disputes the card: the workspace admins read your sentence on the observations behind it, and they can
mark an observation incorrect or withdraw the card. The card's own text stays private to you. Your other
notes are not shown to anyone.

## The practice group table

**See all practice groups** opens the **All practice groups** panel, which lists every practice group in your
workspace as a row: its standing, a ring that splits the group's practices by standing, and a sentence
on what changed in it since the review before.
The rows are ordered by standing, the groups that need attention first; press the **Standing** heading
to reverse the order.

## Opening a group and a practice

Select a row to open the group: what the group is about, what held and what changed in it, your next
step from its newest open feedback, and its practices with their standing and trend. Select a
practice to go one level deeper: the observations behind it, review by review, which you can respond
to; its feedback; and why it matters and what doing it well looks like.

Each panel opens over the one before it, and the page stays visible behind. The short path above a
panel's title says where you are, from the page down to this panel; select a step in it to go back
there, or use Back to return one level.

## Practices across the workspace

**Across the workspace**, under Practice profile in the sidebar, sets your own standing in each
practice group beside how the developers observed in your workspace split across it: how many need
attention, get mixed feedback or are going well. It counts developers and never names one. A group
shows a split only while at least five developers other than you have a standing in it and at least
five have none yet; otherwise it shows only how many developers were observed. When one of the three
standings holds fewer than five, it shows only how many have a standing and how many have none yet.
Your own standing is always shown as a word.

Before a group shows the workspace, it asks where you think you stand. One press answers it, **Skip**
moves on, and **Show all without estimating** answers every group at once. Your estimates stay in your
browser: nobody else sees them, and they never enter a workspace figure. **Start over** asks again.

Turn **Show the workspace** off to see only your own standings, or **Ask me first** off to see every
group straight away; both are remembered in this browser. The range above the figures switches between
this term, the last 30 days and the last 90 days. A group's name opens that group on your Practice
profile.

## When nothing has been reviewed yet

Before the first review of your work, every practice counts as not observed yet, the summary is empty
and there is no feedback to show. The first review fills the page in.

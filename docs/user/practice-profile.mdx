Use your Practice profile to read feedback, choose a next step, and check the evidence behind your practice standing.
Other developers cannot see it.
[Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy.mdx#ai-processing-and-visibility) lists who else can.

The profile uses practice review results about your own pull requests, merge requests, issues, conversations, and documents.
It never compares you with other developers.

## Opening your Practice profile

1. Select a workspace.
2. Under **Dashboards**, select **Practice profile**.

A workspace that reviews practices also opens here by default.
A workspace that does not review practices has no Practice profile and opens on [Activity](./activity.mdx).

## Your feedback

1. Select **Open** to see feedback that still needs a response or more reviewed work.
2. Read the evidence and next step on a card.
3. Decide what to change, or record your response below the card.

For example, feedback about an unclear purpose can help you improve the description of your next pull request.

Each card describes one repeated pattern in your work.
It states what reviews observed, on which work, and when.
It also gives one next step to try.
The tabs show cards newest first:

- **Newest** shows the two newest open cards.
- **Open** shows each open card.
- **Resolved and closed** shows each resolved or closed card.
- **All** shows each card, including withdrawn cards.

A card resolves in one of two ways:

- **By your work.** Three consecutive pieces of reviewed work with no problem on the practice resolve the card. It reads *Resolved by the work* and names that work. Work that shows the problem again resets the count. The line under the next step shows the count.
- **By you.** Your response can mark the card *Marked as addressed* or *Marked as not applicable*, with the date.

To record or undo your response:

1. Under **Your response**, select **Addressed** or **Not applicable**.
2. To undo your response, select it again.

The card opens again when you undo the response.
If your workspace changes a practice after a review wrote its card, the card closes without resolution.
It reads *Closed*, gives the date and reason, and stops counting work that shows no problem on the practice.
A closed card stays on the page for 30 days after closure.
A newer card for the same practice replaces the open card, so each practice has at most one open card.

A workspace admin can withdraw a card with incorrect words.
If you already saw the card, a recent withdrawal appears under **All** as a short notice, without the card’s text.
A card you had not seen never appears.
A withdrawn card accepts no new response.
It resolved nothing and says nothing about your standing.
The observations stay unchanged.

Later reviews and Heph do not receive the withdrawn text.
If the admin restores the card, it returns as it was.

To rate a card:

1. Select **Helpful** or **Not helpful**.
2. If you selected **Helpful**, add a note about what worked. The note is optional.
3. If you selected **Not helpful**, choose a reason.
4. Describe what was wrong in one or two sentences.

The reason **Not accurate** disputes the card.
Workspace admins read your sentence on the observations behind it.
They can mark an observation incorrect or withdraw the card.
The card text stays private to you.
Nobody else sees your other notes.

## Reading the top of the page

The header explains what Hephaestus reviews and how feedback resolves.
The chip beside the title reads **Latest review** with the date and reviewed work.
It can instead say that the review stopped before it finished.
While a review runs, the chip reads **Review running** and names the work.
Both states open [Reviews of your work](#reviews-of-your-work).

A review counts here only after it records a result about your work.
Until then, it appears neither on the chip nor in the list.
Before your first review, the chip reads **No review yet**.

A ring counts your practices by standing:

- **Going well**
- **Mixed feedback**
- **Needs attention**
- **Nothing to report yet**
- **Not observed yet**

**See all practice groups** opens the table of practice groups.
Under the header, Heph summarizes what changed since the review before the latest one:

- **What is holding up well** lists practices that your work continued to meet. Each shows how many pieces of work met it and any feedback your work resolved.
- **What needs your attention** lists at most two practices you can act on today. These include new feedback, and feedback that your work started to answer but a newer review raised again. Each row names the event and the work. **Read the feedback** opens that card below.
- **What changed** lists changes in practice or group standing, first observations, and changes in trends. The paragraph names the most important changes and counts the rest. A button such as **Show the three changes** expands them.
- The latest reviewed work appears by kind.

The summary describes recent evidence, not your ability.
It has no scores, percentages, or ranking.

## Reviews of your work

The title chip opens the reviews that recorded a result about your work, newest first and grouped by day.
A review that still runs may not appear yet, because a review appears only after it records a result about your work.
The newest review has the **Latest** tag.
A review that someone requested has the **Requested** tag.
Its results stay visible here, but they do not move a trend, resolve a card, or start a card.

Each row names the work and links to the original.
It starts with the practices to improve: the first two by name, then a count of the rest.
If there are none, it says *Nothing to improve*, or it says that the review stopped early or still runs.
Below that, it counts each practice once as met, not applicable, or undetermined.
It then counts the practices the review reached, including those with no recorded result about you.

For feedback on GitLab, **Read the feedback on the merge request** opens the comment.
Elsewhere, the row counts the pieces of feedback that reached you.
**Show** filters the list by kind of work.
**Timeframe** filters it to the last 7, 30, or 90 days.
**All time** is the initial value.
With either filter on, no row shows the **Latest** tag, because the newest included review might not be your latest.

**View earlier reviews** loads older reviews.
After the whole list loads without filters, later reviews of the same work show their sequence, such as *2nd review*.
**Request review** appears beside a row when you may request another review as author, assignee, or workspace admin.

**Open review** opens a review.
It shows the link to the original work, the date, and the number of practices reached.
It also shows **Request review** when you may request a review.
Two tabs follow:

- **Every practice** lists the practices behind the review. Each shows one line per observation or explains why it sent no feedback. During a review, practices appear as the review reaches them.
- **What we noticed** lists all recorded events about the work in order. It shows what happened, how the review noticed it, and whether a review started.

In **Every practice**, these filters apply:

- **Group** selects one practice group.
- **Reviews when** selects a review occasion, such as new commits pushed.
- **Practice** filters names by the text you enter.

## The practice group table

**See all practice groups** opens the **All practice groups** panel.
Each row shows a workspace practice group, its standing, and a ring that splits its practices by standing.
A sentence describes what changed since the previous review.
The rows appear by standing, with groups that need attention first.

1. To reverse the order, select the **Standing** heading.

## Opening a group and a practice

1. Select a group row.
2. To see more detail, select a practice.

The group panel describes the group, which practices were met, and what changed.
It shows the next step from its newest open feedback, and each practice’s standing and trend.
The practice panel shows observations by review, with response controls, and feedback.
It also explains why the practice matters and what work that meets it looks like.

A standing shows how many pieces of your work it is read from, for example "from 3 pieces of work".
Your latest work counts most, and older work counts less.
If fewer than three pieces of work back a standing, its sentence calls it an early read.
The next piece of work can change an early read.

Each panel opens over the previous panel, with the page visible behind it.
The short path above the title shows where you are, from the page to the panel.

1. To go to a position on the path, select it.
2. To go back one level, select **Back**.

## Practices across the workspace

Practices across the workspace shows where the developers in your workspace stand in each practice group.
The page counts developers and never names one.
It is a view of the workspace, not your profile.
Your own standings, trends, and next step are in your Practice profile.

1. In the sidebar, under **Practice profile**, select **Across the workspace**.
   If the sidebar shows only icons, select it in the **Practice profile** menu.

A developer has a standing when their reviewed work met or did not meet a practice in at least one practice group.
A developer whose reviews found only practices that did not apply, or left them undetermined, has no standing yet.

Four figures come first:

- Pieces of your work reviewed.
- Practices going well.
- Practices needing attention.
- Your open feedback.

The first three use the range that you select.

Each figure shows your own value on a small axis.
Your marker shows your value.
When at least six other developers have a standing, the figure also shows the typical range as a grey band.

To find the typical range, Hephaestus sorts the developers with a standing in the selected range by their value.
The band covers the middle half.
A quarter of the developers are below it, and a quarter are above it.
The text under the figures gives the number of developers, if at least three developers other than you have a standing.

Open feedback counts what your Practice profile shows as open now.
The range that you select does not change it.
It counts you and every other developer that this page counts, reviewed or not.
Its typical range is the middle half of these developers, when at least six others are here.

**All practice groups** lists each group with a bar.
The bar shows how many developers need attention, get mixed feedback, are going well, or have no standing yet in the group.
Under the bar, the page shows how many developers the bar counts.
**You** marks your own part.
A legend above the table explains each mark.

Each developer counts with their current standing, the standing that their Practice profile shows.
So your marker always agrees with your Practice profile.
The range that you select does not change the bars.

Each part of a bar holds at least four developers.
So each part stands for at least three developers other than you.
Everyone in the workspace sees the same bars.

If one part would hold fewer, the parts are held back.
The bar is then one grey bar with the number of developers that it counts and **Split held back** under it.
If even that number would hold fewer than four developers, the whole bar is held back.
A held back bar is a dashed track with the reason under it.

The number of developers with a standing shows when at least three developers other than you have one.
The parts of a practice’s bar are also held back if its split and its group’s split together would single out fewer than three developers.

**Open group** opens a panel with the group’s bar.
It also shows the bar for each practice in the group.
To close the panel, select **Close**.

To see your standing, trend, and next step in the group, select **Open in your Practice profile**.
Your Practice profile opens with the group open.
To see your observations and feedback for a practice, select **Open in your Practice profile** on its row.
Your Practice profile opens with the practice open.

The range beside the four figures changes only the three figures that use it.
It has three values:

- The last 30 days, where the page opens.
- The last 90 days.
- All time.

## When nothing has been reviewed yet

Before the first review, each practice counts as **Not observed yet**.
The summary is empty and no feedback appears.
The first review with a recorded result fills the page.
To request a review of eligible work, use **Request review** on [Activity](./activity.mdx#request-a-review).

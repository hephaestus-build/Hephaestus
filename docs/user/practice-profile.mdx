Your Practice profile shows where your recent work stands on the practices your workspace cares about,
what held and what changed since the review before the latest one, and the feedback written from it.
Other developers cannot see it; [Your data in Hephaestus](./privacy.mdx#ai-processing-and-visibility)
lists who else can. It rests on practice feedback about your own pull requests, merge requests,
issues, conversations and documents, never on comparisons with other developers.

## Opening it

Sign in, select a workspace and choose **Practice profile** in the sidebar under Dashboards.

## Reading the top of the page

The header says what Hephaestus reviews and how a piece of feedback resolves, and **Latest run** names
the latest review and the work it ran on. A ring counts your practices by standing — going well, mixed feedback, needs
attention, nothing to report yet, or not observed yet — and **See all practice groups** opens the
table of every practice group.

Under the header, Heph sums up what happened since the review before the latest one:

- **What is holding up well** — the practices you kept showing, each with how many pieces of work it
  held across, and any feedback your work resolved.
- **What needs your attention** — at most two practices you can do something about today: feedback
  your work had started to answer and a newer review raised again, and feedback that is new. Each
  row says what happened and on which piece of work, and **Read the feedback** takes you straight to
  that card below.
- **What changed** — a practice or group that moved to another standing, one seen for the first
  time, a trend that turned. The paragraph names the most important moves and counts the rest;
  **Show the changes** unfolds them.
- The work the latest review looked at, listed by kind.

The summary describes recent evidence, not your ability. There are no scores, no percentages and no
ranking.

## Your feedback

Each card is one pattern that recurred across your work: what was seen, on which pieces of work and
when, and one next step to try. The tabs above the cards split them, newest first: **Newest** shows
the two newest open cards, **Open** every open card, **Resolved** every closed card, and **All** every
card.

A card resolves in one of two ways:

- **By your work.** Once three pieces of your work in a row come back clean on the practice, the card
  reads *Resolved by the work* and names the pieces of work that did it. A piece of work that shows the
  problem again starts the count over; the line under the next step keeps the count.
- **By you.** Under **Your response**, choose **Addressed** or **Not applicable** on the card; it then
  reads *Marked as addressed* or *Marked as not applicable* with the day you did. Press it again to
  take it back, and the card opens again.

If your workspace changes a practice after a card about it was written, the card closes and says so. A
closed card stays on the page for 30 days after it closed. A newer card about the same practice
replaces the open one, so you never have two open cards about one habit.

Rate a card **Helpful** or **Not helpful**. Helpful opens an optional note; Not helpful asks for a reason
and a sentence on what was off, so the feedback can improve.

## The practice group table

**See all practice groups** opens the **All practice groups** panel, which lists every practice group in your
workspace as a row: its standing, a ring that splits the group's practices by standing, and a sentence
on what changed in it since the review before.
The rows are ordered by standing, the groups that need attention first; press the **Standing** heading
to reverse the order.

## Opening a group and a practice

Select a row to open the group: what the group is about, what held and what changed in it, your next
step from its newest open feedback, and its practices with their standing and trend. Select a
practice to go one level deeper: the observations behind it, review by review, which you can respond
to; its feedback; and why it matters and what doing it well looks like.

Each panel opens over the one before it, and the page stays visible behind. The short path above a
panel's title says where you are, from the page down to this panel; select a step in it to go back
there, or use Back to return one level.

## When nothing has been reviewed yet

Before the first review of your work, every practice counts as not observed yet, the summary is empty
and there is no feedback to show. The first review fills the page in.

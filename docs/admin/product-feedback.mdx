Instance administrators read submissions under **Instance administration → Product feedback → Inbox**.
Product feedback is separate from practice feedback and stays on this instance.

## Read and resolve submissions

**Inbox** lists ideas, bug reports, and feedback.
Each item identifies its sender, workspace, and the Hephaestus release that received it.
It includes the page and browser only if the sender chose to attach them.
**Mark resolved** removes an item from the open list.
**Reopen** returns it to that list.
Neither action notifies anyone.

To receive email about new submissions:

1. In your personal settings, opt into product-feedback email.

To handle a report:

1. Sign in.
2. Use **Inbox** to read the report text or attached context.
3. Follow your team's process for reports.
4. If necessary, follow up with the sender.
5. Resolve or reopen the item.

Emails are sent as soon as the submission commits, subject to capacity and relay availability.
They never include report text or attached context.
Only currently active instance admins with verified email addresses receive them.
Resolution is private administration, not a reply.

An idea or bug that concerns every instance is worth reporting to the [Hephaestus project](https://github.com/hephaestus-build/Hephaestus/issues).
Nothing reports it automatically.

1. Before you create a public issue, remove private details.

Product feedback can identify questions worth study.
Collection for product improvement does not authorize reuse of identifiable reports or quotations as research data.
A general research opt-in does not permit reuse of every submission for a different purpose.

Before research collection or reuse, agree the research purpose, lawful basis, participant notice, retention, and safeguards with your institution.

## Check that it worked

After you resolve a submission, it leaves the open inbox.
Select the resolved list to find it again.
Reopen it to return it to the open inbox.

## Handle personal data

Submissions link to accounts and stay on your instance.
Users can attach the displayed page path and their browser’s user agent.
Submissions never attach query parameters, page contents, or logs.
They always record the Hephaestus release that received the report.
Drafts never reach the server.
Product feedback has a limit of one submission per account per minute.

Account erasure and workspace purge remove the corresponding submissions.
These operations cannot recall email that a relay already accepted.
There is no automatic expiry by age.
Include product feedback in your retention and rights-request procedures.
The [personal-data map](dsms/personal-data-map.md) describes erasure coverage.

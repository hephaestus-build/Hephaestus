# Product feedback and surveys

Instance administrators read submissions and publish surveys under **Administration → Product
feedback**, which has two pages: **Inbox** for feedback and bug reports, and **Surveys** for
publishing surveys and reading their responses. Workspace administrator access alone does not grant
access to either.

## Publish a survey

On **Surveys**, choose **Create survey** and pick an audience: one workspace or all workspaces.
State the decision you want to inform in the purpose; members see it with the invitation.

Short surveys get answered: aim for one to three questions, closed questions first and one optional
free-text question last. Questions are optional unless you mark them required. Each question is
free text, single choice, multiple choice, a 1–5 rating with a label for each end of the scale, or
a 0–10 recommendation question scored as a Net Promoter Score. Include “Not applicable” as a choice
when appropriate. **Preview** shows the survey exactly as members will see it.

Times use your device's local timezone. A blank start means publication time; a blank end means
no scheduled expiry. Set an end to avoid stale invitations. Questions cannot be edited after
publishing; publish a new survey if the wording needs changing.

Members see open surveys under **Feedback** in the app header. The first time an account is shown
an invitation it also gets one short, dismissible notice; nothing opens by itself, and the notice is
never repeated. From a survey's actions:

- **Pause** makes a survey unavailable; **Resume** restores availability within the original
  schedule. Resuming neither extends the end time nor resets responses or declines. Availability
  is checked when a response is submitted, but pausing does not cancel submissions already in
  progress.
- **End now** closes the survey at the current time. Members are no longer invited; responses
  stay.
- **Delete** removes the survey and every response and decline it collected. This cannot be undone.

## Read survey results

Open a survey from the table to see who was invited, who responded and who declined, a summary per
question, and every response with the submitting account and workspace. **Export CSV** downloads
the responses.

Voluntary responses are not a representative satisfaction measurement. Declines are not negative
ratings, and skipped questions are not zeroes. Do not reuse product responses for research.

## Act on feedback

**Inbox** lists feedback and bug reports, open ones first. Each shows who sent it, from which
workspace and page, and their browser and Hephaestus version when the report included them. **Mark
resolved** moves an item out of the open list; **Reopen** brings it back. Nobody is notified either
way, and there are no submission notifications, so check the inbox regularly and follow your team's
process for acting on reports.

## Handle personal data

Submissions are linked to accounts and stored on your instance. Users can choose to attach the
displayed page path and their browser's user agent; query parameters, page contents and logs are
never attached, and the Hephaestus release that received the report is recorded either way.
Unsubmitted drafts are lost on refresh, workspace change or sign-out. Feedback is limited to one
submission per account per minute. Each account can respond to a survey once; a survey records when
its invitation was first shown to an account, so the results page can report a completion rate.

Account erasure and workspace purge remove the corresponding submissions. There is no automatic
age-based deletion: include product feedback in your retention and rights-request procedures.
The [personal-data map](dsms/personal-data-map.md) describes erasure coverage.

# Product feedback and surveys

Instance administrators read submissions and publish surveys under **Administration → Product
feedback**, which has two pages: **Inbox** for ideas, bug reports and feedback, and **Surveys** for
publishing surveys and reading their responses. Workspace administrator access alone does not grant
access to either. Members see the readers described as *the Hephaestus team*.

## Publish a survey

On **Surveys**, choose **Create survey**. Write a title and an introduction — members read the
introduction before the first question, so say why you are asking and what the answers will change.
Pick an audience: one workspace or all workspaces.

Where the instance names a [research organisation](legal-pages.mdx#the-optional-research-question),
the composer also asks for a **purpose**:

- **Product** surveys are read by the instance administrators — *the Hephaestus team* to members —
  and are never research.
- **Research** surveys are part of the study run by the named organisation. They are offered only to
  members whose research consent is a current grant for that organisation, are labelled as research
  on screen with a link to the setting that withdraws consent, and their answers are that study's
  data. A member who leaves the study stops being asked; switching the programme off or renaming it
  hides every research survey published for the old name. The purpose cannot be changed after
  publishing.

Without a research organisation, every survey is a product survey and the choice is not shown.

Questions are optional unless you mark them required; keep most optional, because someone who
cannot answer a required question leaves. Members see *(optional)* after every optional question,
so the required ones are the unmarked ones. Each question is free text, single choice, multiple
choice, a 1–5 rating with a label for each end of the scale, or a 0–10 recommendation question
scored as a Net Promoter Score, which also turns off the keyboard answer shortcuts for the whole
survey. Include “Not applicable” as a choice when appropriate, or allow **Something else** so a
member can write an answer you did not list. Ask about what members did, not what they would do.
**Preview** opens the very dialog members get, on your draft, and sends nothing; it refuses a draft
that could not be published, for the same reasons.

The schedule is entered in your device's timezone. A survey with no end stays open until you end
it. Questions cannot be edited after publishing; publish a new survey if the wording needs changing.

How members are invited and how they answer or decline is described in
[Feedback and surveys](/user/product-feedback). From a survey's actions:

- **Pause** makes a survey unavailable; **Resume** restores availability within the original
  schedule. Resuming neither extends the end time nor resets responses or declines. A member who
  is mid-answer when you pause is refused on send and keeps their draft.
- **End now** closes an open survey at the current time. Members are no longer invited; responses
  stay. A scheduled survey has not started, so it cannot be ended; delete it instead.
- **Delete** removes the survey and every response and decline it collected. This cannot be undone.

## Invite by email

Creating or editing a survey never starts an email campaign. From its results page, preview the
email audience and explicitly request invitations. Only people who opted into the matching product
or research survey emails are eligible; research invitations additionally require current consent for
the survey's research organisation. Each recipient must still belong to an eligible workspace and
have an active account with a verified address. One person receives one invitation across workspaces.

Each request queues at most 1,000 recipients. Repeat the action to continue a larger audience;
initial invitations already accepted by the relay are not requested again. Eligibility is checked again when sending or
retrying. Pausing cancels pending invitations; resuming does not revive them. Closing or deleting the
survey, answering, declining, withdrawing consent or leaving the audience prevents queued delivery.

The email audience card distinguishes:

- **Eligible now**: current subscribers who can still answer this survey.
- **Already requested**: people for whom an email invitation was requested, including cancelled
  requests. A request is not proof of delivery.
- **Accepted by relay**: initial invitations accepted by SMTP, not opened or answered. Reminders
  do not increase this count.
- **Remaining**: eligible people who have never been requested, or whose initial invitation was
  cancelled before relay acceptance and can be explicitly requested again.

The result of the send action reports how many invitations were newly queued in that batch; it is
not a count of messages still waiting. Email requests and acceptance are separate from the in-app
invitation counts used for completion rates. No tracking pixels are added and no answers are sent
in email.

### One optional reminder

**Send one reminder** starts off for each send action. If selected, one reminder can be scheduled
for each new recipient at least 72 hours after the relay accepts their initial invitation. It is
not a repeating campaign: answering, declining, unsubscribing, leaving the audience or losing research
consent prevents the reminder. The survey must still be open, and email requests expire seven days
after they were made or when the survey closes, whichever comes first.

Pausing cancels both pending initial invitations and pending reminders. Resuming does not revive
those requests or remove their **Already requested** history. After resuming, a fresh send action can
explicitly request a cancelled initial invitation that the relay never accepted. It cannot repeat an
accepted initial invitation or revive that invitation's cancelled reminder.

### End-of-survey summaries

Instance administrators can separately opt into **Survey summaries** in their email notification
settings. When a survey ends, its summary contains aggregate counts of people shown the invitation
in the app, responses and declines, plus a link to the administrator console. It contains no
individual answers. Subscribing to survey invitations does not subscribe an administrator to summaries.

Only active instance administrators with verified email addresses receive summaries, and eligibility
is checked again before sending. Summaries are prepared only for surveys that ended within the last
seven days; older surveys are not backfilled after downtime. A summary is prepared once for a survey's end time, not once per response or each time someone
changes their subscription. Changing the end time invalidates a queued summary for the old schedule
and allows a new summary when the revised end is reached.

## Read survey results

Open a survey from the table to see who was invited, who responded and who declined, a summary per
question, and every response with the submitting account and workspace. **Export CSV** downloads
the responses, up to 10,000 rows.

Voluntary responses are not a representative satisfaction measurement. Declines are not negative
ratings, and skipped questions are not zeroes. Do not reuse product responses for research; a
research survey's results page reminds you whose data they are.

## Act on feedback

**Inbox** lists ideas, bug reports and feedback. Each shows who sent it, from which workspace, the
page and browser when the sender chose to attach them, and the Hephaestus release that received it.
**Mark resolved** moves an item out of the open list; **Reopen** brings it back. Nobody is notified
either way. In your personal settings, opt in to product-feedback email for new submissions,
either immediately or as a daily digest. Digests run after 08:00 UTC for the previous UTC calendar
day, skip empty windows and combine at most seven days after downtime. They never include report
text or attached context: sign in to read it. Only currently active instance administrators with a
verified email address receive these emails. Follow your team's process for acting on reports. An idea or a bug that concerns every instance is worth passing
on to the [Hephaestus project](https://github.com/hephaestus-build/Hephaestus/issues); nothing does
that for you.

## Handle personal data

Submissions are linked to accounts and stored on your instance. Users can choose to attach the
displayed page path and their browser's user agent; query parameters, page contents and logs are
never attached, and the Hephaestus release that received the report is recorded either way.
Drafts never reach the server. Feedback is limited to one submission per account per minute. Each
account can respond to a survey once; a survey records when its invitation was first shown to an
account, so the results page can report a completion rate.

Research survey answers are collected under the research consent and stay with the survey after a
member withdraws; removing them means erasing the account or deleting the survey. Account erasure and
workspace purge remove the corresponding submissions. Account erasure also removes that account's
survey email-invitation records and clears its requester attribution on other people's invitations.
Deleting a survey removes its invitation records alongside its responses; workspace purge removes
records belonging to that workspace. These operations cannot recall email already accepted by a relay.
There is no automatic age-based deletion of retained invitation records: include them and product
feedback in your retention and rights-request procedures. The
[personal-data map](dsms/personal-data-map.md) describes erasure coverage.

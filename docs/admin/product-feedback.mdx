# Product feedback and surveys

Instance administrators manage surveys and read submissions under **Administration → Product
feedback**. Workspace administrator access alone does not grant access to this inbox.

## Publish a survey

Open **Surveys** and choose an audience: one workspace or all workspaces. State the decision you
want to inform in the purpose. Keep questions short and focused; they are optional by default.

Use free text for context, single choice for distinct alternatives, or a 1–5 rating. Explain what
1 and 5 mean in the question, or use single choice to label every scale point. Include “Not
applicable” when appropriate. An optional free-text follow-up lets people explain their choice.

Times use your device's local timezone. A blank start means publication time; a blank end means
no scheduled expiry. Set an end to avoid stale invitations. Questions cannot be edited after
publishing; publish a new survey if the wording needs changing.

Available surveys appear in the app header without opening a dialog. **Pause survey** makes
a survey unavailable; **Resume survey** restores availability within the original schedule. Resuming
neither extends the end time nor resets responses or declines. Availability is checked when a
response is submitted, but pausing does not cancel submissions already in progress.

## Read and act on submissions

The **Feedback** tab contains feedback and bug reports. **Survey responses** contains answers and
explicit declines, with the submitting account shown. Check the inbox regularly;
there are no submission notifications. Follow your team's process for acting on reports.

Voluntary responses are not a representative satisfaction measurement. Declines are not negative
ratings, and skipped questions are not zeroes. Do not reuse product responses for research.

## Handle personal data

Submissions are linked to accounts and stored on your instance. Users can choose to attach the
displayed page path; query parameters, page contents and logs are not attached. Unsubmitted drafts
are lost on refresh, workspace change or sign-out. Feedback is limited to one submission per account
per minute. Each account can respond to a survey once.

Account erasure and workspace purge remove the corresponding submissions. There is no automatic
age-based deletion: include product feedback in your retention and rights-request procedures.
The [personal-data map](dsms/personal-data-map.md) describes erasure coverage.

A practice is a habit Hephaestus reviews work against. Where a practice lives decides who owns it and
what changing it affects.

Before creating or adapting an entry, use [Writing effective practices](./writing-practices) to define
its evidence boundary and test its judgments against realistic cases.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    accTitle: The three places a practice definition lives
    accDescr: Hephaestus ships bundled defaults. An instance administrator customizes them into the instance catalog, which decides what workspaces may adopt. A workspace administrator adopts an independent copy and reviews later updates before applying them.
    Bundled[Bundled defaults<br/>shipped with Hephaestus] -->|upgrade, unless customized| Catalog[Instance catalog<br/>what workspaces may adopt]
    Catalog -->|adoption, by an administrator| Copy[Workspace practice<br/>an independent copy]
    Copy -->|never flows back| Catalog
```

| Scope | Owner | Changing it affects |
|---|---|---|
| Bundled defaults | Hephaestus releases | Every instance that has not customized the entry |
| Instance catalog | Instance administrator | What workspaces may adopt next, and what updates existing adopters can review |
| Workspace practice | Workspace administrator | Reviews in that one workspace |

The arrow only ever points right. A release can update an instance entry nobody customized, and an
instance change alters what is on offer — but **nothing rewrites a workspace practice**. That is the
whole point of adoption: a workspace can rely on its practices not changing under it.

## Adopt a practice into a workspace

In **Workspace administration → Practice setup**, select **Show catalog**. Available practices appear
beside the ones you already have, organized into groups.

Select one to read it before deciding. You get the habit and why it matters first, then what good
looks like, then — under disclosures — what the practice reads, how it decides, and what it measures
before the model sees anything.

:::note Adding never starts sending feedback
A practice Hephaestus can review starts at **Review before sending**. A practice it cannot review
stays **Off** until you connect what it needs to read. Moving a practice to **Send automatically** is
a separate decision you make later, on the **Review** screen.
:::

What you get is a copy. Edit it, move it, rename it, delete it — none of that touches the catalog, and
nothing in the catalog changes it without your approval.

### Review a later update

Open **Workspace administration → Practice setup → Review updates**. Each practice you adopted has
its own proposal when the instance catalog changes. The comparison shows the version you adopted,
your current version, and the offered version, one changed field at a time. A **Conflict** means
both sides changed that field. A **Local edit** means only your workspace changed it; it stays as it
is. Choose **Current** or **Offered** for every field the catalog changed, then accept. Your choice
creates a new practice revision for this workspace only. Earlier observations keep the revision
that judged them. A change to review rules starts a new recurrence chain; guidance-only changes do
not.

You can decline an offered version. It does not change the practice or create a revision, and the
same version is not offered again. A later different version can be reviewed. If the original
adopted content was not stored by an older release, **Comparison base** says whether Hephaestus
found a matching bundled version or used your current definition. Review that comparison with
extra care; it cannot recover content that was never stored.

### Adopt a whole group

A group's action adopts everything in it that the workspace is missing. Hephaestus shows the full plan
first — what it will add, what is already there, and anything it cannot add — and then applies all of
it or none of it. If the catalog or your workspace changed while you were reading, the plan is
refused and you review the current one.

### Remove a group

Removing a group asks what should happen to the practices in it:

- **Keep practices unassigned** — they stay in the workspace, without a group.
- **Delete group and practices** — this also deletes their observations, and cannot be undone.

If you kept them, the catalog offers **Restore group**, which puts the matching unassigned copies back
without touching any edits you made to them.

### Adopt something again

Delete an adopted practice and the catalog includes it again immediately. Adoption is keyed on the
practice's slug in your workspace, so you cannot end up with two copies of the same practice.

## Read the badges

A badge says how your copy stands against the entry it came from. All of them are informational:
**a copy never changes on its own**, edited or not.

**In a workspace**, comparing your copy against the catalog:

| Badge | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Same as the catalog** | Your copy matches the catalog | Nothing |
| **Edited here** | Your definition differs from the version you adopted and from the catalog | Check **Review updates** for any later catalog proposal |
| **Catalog changed, yours did not** | Your copy still equals its adopted base, but the catalog moved on | Open **Practice updates** and choose fields to accept or decline |
| **Update declined** | You declined this exact catalog version | Nothing. A different version can be offered later |
| **No longer in the catalog** | The source was excluded or removed | Nothing. Your copy keeps working |

**On the instance**, comparing the catalog against the bundled defaults:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Customized on this instance** | You edited it; the bundled definition has not moved |
| **Hephaestus update available** | You edited it and a release changed the bundled definition |
| **No Hephaestus default** | You created it here |
| **Removed from Hephaestus defaults** | A release dropped it; your customization still applies |

An update never replaces a customization silently. Open the entry to compare its bundled base,
your current definition, and the new bundled version. Choose fields to accept, or decline this
version. If you accept only some fields, the others stay as you wrote them. **Use Hephaestus version**
still discards the entire customization; it is not the update review action. The comparison includes
guidance and declared feedback-delivery behavior, not only review criteria.

## How reliable a practice is

Nobody has measured how often any practice is right. Each one carries its author's description of
what it checks; that description has not been tested against real work, and the practice says so
under **What it reads**. This is why a practice starts by asking you to approve each piece of
feedback — keep it there until you have watched it on your own work.

## Curate what the instance offers

In **Instance administration → Practice catalog** you decide what workspaces may adopt.

- **Customize** an entry to change its definition for this instance.
- **Stop offering** an entry to take it off the menu. Workspaces that already adopted it keep their
  copies and notice nothing.
- **Use Hephaestus version** discards your customization and returns to the bundled definition.
- **Use Hephaestus order** discards custom positions.

Excluding a group also removes its practices from what workspaces may adopt.

## Where practices come from on a new instance

At startup, any workspace with no recorded catalog installation receives the whole catalog once. On a
fresh instance that means the workspaces provisioned at boot arrive already stocked; you adopt to add
what you passed over, and to add back anything you deleted.

This page states what Hephaestus is designed for.
Read it before you decide how your organization uses Hephaestus.

## Intended purpose

Hephaestus is an AI mentor for software engineering practices.
It gives developers practice feedback on their own work.
The feedback is formative and self-directed.
It helps a developer see how they apply an engineering practice, and what to do next.
Developers choose what to do with it.

## Uses outside the intended purpose

Hephaestus is not designed for these uses:

- Grading a person.
- Appraising the performance of a person.
- Ranking people.
- Deciding the allocation of work or other resources to a person.
- Deciding the pay, promotion, or employment of a person.

A practice result describes one piece of work against one practice.
It is not a measure of a person.
Do not use a result, a standing, or feedback as an input to such a decision.

## Counts of people

A view that counts people without naming them shows a count only from 5 people.
This applies to members and to staff: workspace admins, workspace owners and instance admins.
The floor is fixed. No setting changes it.

- A count of 1 to 4 people is not shown. A count of zero is shown.
- A split, such as developers by standing, shows all its parts or only its total.
  If one part holds 1 to 4 people, or everyone is in one part, only the total shows.
  The view does not say which of the two applies.
- A figure from a group of people, such as a typical range, needs at least 5 of them.
- **Practices across the workspace** counts each developer with reviewed work in the period and a standing in a practice.
  Hiding a member does not change who it counts.
  The review scope, an ended membership and the groups that the page shows do not change it either.
  An erasure or a restriction of processing, which the person asks for, removes them.
  Bots are not counted, because their work is not reviewed.
- The page says that standings rest on earlier reviews only when at least 5 developers have such standings.
- **Open feedback** shows only the reader's own figure.
  Another person's open feedback depends on their membership and the review scope.
  So the page shows no range for it.

Some counts stay exact.
The reader already sees the same people by name, so the count tells them nothing more:

- The members of a workspace or a team, as their member lists show them.
- The people in the practice review scope.
- The authors of the pull requests that a named person reviewed.

Some counts are not shown at all:

- The people that the public activity page leaves out, and the contributors that it does not name.
- The members who opted out of Slack message use, and the messages stored for each Slack channel.

### What the floor does not stop

The floor limits what one view tells. A reader who compares two views can still learn something about one person.

- **Drift between reviews.** A new review changes a person's standing, so the same count before and after it can differ by one person.
- **Suspension.** A suspended account stops the processing of that person's data, so their standings leave the counts.
- **Repository monitoring.** When a repository that only one person works in stops being monitored, that person's observations from it stop counting.
- **Practice settings.** A practice's reviewer and criteria decide which observations count, so a change can change who is counted.
  The group that a practice sits in decides how a group standing reads.
  Sometimes only one person has a standing in that practice.
  Then a comparison before and after the change can tell something about that person.
- **Feedback mode.** Observations that a review records while a practice's feedback mode is **Off** do not count toward a standing.
- **Corrections.** A corrected observation changes the standing of the person whose work it is about.

A release record will close these cases.
It will compare each new figure with the previous one, and release a change only when it concerns at least 5 people.
Until it ships, do not compare views over time or across these settings to learn about one person.

Research exports keep their own rules. See [Research program](./research-program.mdx).

## Per-person views for admins

The workspace setting **Per-person results for admins** controls what workspace admins see.

- For a new workspace, the setting is off.
  Admins see results, problems and feedback on practice pages and in conversations with no names.
  Each result still shows the work it is about, and the provider shows who wrote that work.
  The Practice reviews lists have no **Developer** filter, and the server refuses a filter by person.
- Feedback on the work always names its developer.
  Hephaestus posts it to the developer on their work, and an admin approves it.
- Only a workspace owner can turn the setting on, after a confirmation.
- Any workspace admin or owner can turn it off, in one step.
- Hephaestus records each change in the configuration audit of the workspace, with the actor and the time.

A workspace that ran practice reviews before the setting existed keeps it on after the upgrade.
The upgrade records one audit entry for each such workspace.
To turn it off, follow [Choose whether admins see results per person](/user/workspace#choose-whether-admins-see-results-per-person).

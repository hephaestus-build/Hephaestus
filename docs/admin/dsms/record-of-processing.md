# Hephaestus — Record of Processing Activities (Art. 30 GDPR)

This page is a template for the Art. 30 record of a Hephaestus deployment.
Each section maps to one Art. 30 element.
Replace each placeholder with the facts of your deployment, and keep the record in your own governance system.

The pointers name the pages that describe what Hephaestus itself does.
Code verifies product behavior, not signed agreements, live settings or operational measures.

## Controller and contact (Art. 30(1)(a))

`[controller name, address, representative, privacy contact, DPO where applicable]`.
Name any joint controllers and their actual arrangement.
An admin role alone does not establish Art. 26 status.

## Purpose and description (Art. 30(1)(b))

`[each purpose, for each group of data subjects]`.
Describe the enabled features: practice review of connected repositories, Heph conversations, the weekly digest, Activity, public activity pages, and research use, where you enable them.
[Practice review](../practice-review.mdx), [Public activity pages](../public-activity.mdx) and [Legal pages](../legal-pages.mdx#the-optional-research-question) describe what each feature processes.

Include the people whose work Hephaestus mirrors without a sign-in, and Slack participants where you connect Slack.
